## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Module mission

Give a Quebec construction company a **single register of its rentals**, in two complementary forms:

- **Equipment rental**: keep a catalog of rentable material (excavator, crane, aerial lift, scaffolding, generator, tools…), build rental contracts for clients with detailed lines and taxed totals (GST 5%, QST 9.975%), manage deposits, then record returns via an inspection comparing the condition before and after.
- **Labor lending**: declare which employees are "lendable" to another contractor (with trade, hourly and daily rates), build lending contracts, and log the hours worked in them.

Concretely, the module answers questions such as:

- "Which equipment is available for rental today, and at what rate?"
- "How much is this contract worth, taxes included, and has the deposit been received?"
- "Is this equipment back, and in what condition? Are there damage charges to bill?"
- "Which employees can I lend next week, and how many hours have they logged on the current contract?"
- "Rent or buy for this excavator?" (AI assistant)

### 1.2 What the module does (verified against the code)

- **Equipment catalog**: create, list (up to 200 per request), search, filter by category and condition, edit, and delete (soft delete) material records. Each record carries daily, weekly, and monthly rates, a purchase and a replacement value, a deposit, an insurance-required flag and, optionally, a **tiered rate** (automatic discount beyond a threshold of days).
- **Rental contracts**: create (`LOC-#####` number generated by the server), list, search, filter by status, open the detail, change the status, and delete (only in `BROUILLON` or `ANNULE`).
- **Contract lines**: add, edit, and remove equipment in a contract, with quantity, rate, rate type, discount, and checkout/return dates. The server computes each line's amount and recomputes the contract totals (pre-tax, GST, QST, total incl. tax). An **oversell guard** prevents checking out more units than remain (409 error).
- **Returns by inspection**: for each non-returned line, record a return with the condition after, the observed damage, and up to three charges (repair, cleaning, late). These **charges are billable**: they flow into the contract's pre-tax subtotal (thus taxed). When all lines are returned, the contract automatically moves to `RETOURNE`.
- **Labor lending**: configure an employee for rental (availability, status, trade, rates), create lending contracts (`EMP-#####` number), change their status (which automatically syncs the employee's status), and enter hours worked (which add up to the contract's actual-hours counter).
- **Summary tables**: a dashboard (indicators, latest contracts, breakdown by category) and a Statistics tab (breakdown by status, by condition, top-10 clients by revenue).
- **AI assistant**: five tools (equipment recommendation, rent vs. buy, checklist, contract analysis, free-form question) that produce advice written by Claude, charged to the company's AI credits.

### 1.3 What the module does NOT do (important limits)

> **Read this before relying on the module.** Several natural expectations are **not** covered, and some capabilities exist on the server side with no screen to use them.

- **No export, no printing, no file upload, no photo.** There is **no PDF, no CSV, and no printing** anywhere in the module: no printable contract, no release slip, no return receipt, no attachment. The module produces **no** document handed to the client.
- **No invoice in accounting.** Moving a contract to `FACTURE` status only **changes the status**: the amounts stay in the rental tables, no invoice is created in the Accounting module. Posting to billing is manual.
- **A contract's client is just free text.** The creation window does **not** link the contract to a company, a contact, a project, or a person in charge, even though the server can store these links (`client_company_id`, `client_contact_id`, `project_id`, `responsable_id`). Only the **hand-entered name** is kept.
- **Partial editing of contracts.** In a contract's detail, you can change the **status** and the **"Deposit received"** flag, but the creation window (client, dates, duration, location, deposit, conditions) is **not** re-editable afterward from the screen; the equipment of an existing line is **not** editable (only the quantity, rate, type, discount, and dates are).
- **No time-based reservation conflict detection.** Availability is a quantity (total minus units out), not a calendar: the module does not warn that a piece of equipment is already reserved for a future period. It only refuses to **exceed the physical quantity** at checkout time.
- **Labor lending computes no billable total with the screen's rate types.** The automatic amount computation (estimated and billed from hours) exists **only** for an "hourly" rate type that **the dropdown does not offer** (it offers day, week, month, flat). With these four types, the "Amount" column stays "--" until an amount is set via the API (see 2.6.3 and 4.9).
- **Lending hours do not go into payroll.** The entered hours add up to the lending contract's counter but are **not** propagated to the Payroll / Time-tracking module.
- **No automatic notification** (email, text message, browser notification) on overdue returns, deadlines, or deposits.
- **The AI assistant is blocked in consultation mode.** Since its five endpoints are write requests (POST), a tenant in **consultation mode** (inactive Stripe subscription) **cannot** use them, even though the AI conceptually only reads (see 1.5).

### 1.5 Permissions and roles

The module clearly separates **reading** (open) from **writing** (reserved for certain roles).

| Action | Who can do it |
|--------|---------------|
| **View** (all tabs, all GETs: catalog, contracts, detail, returns, labor, statistics) | Any authenticated user of the tenant (`get_current_user`). |
| **Create / edit / delete** a piece of equipment, a contract, a line, a return, an employee configuration, a lending contract, or hours | Rental write role: **administrator**, **super administrator**, **manager**, **foreman**, or **storekeeper** (`require_tenant_admin_or_role`, `secondary.py:44,49` → `erp_auth.py:720`). An owner whose account carries `is_admin` is always authorized (right re-read on the server, non-forgeable). Otherwise: **403**. |
| **Use the AI assistant** | Any authenticated user **with AI credits** (see 1.7). |

> **Write roles identical to Logistics.** `RENTAL_WRITE_ROLES` equals exactly `LOGISTICS_WRITE_ROLES`: `admin`, `super_admin`, `gestionnaire`, `contremaitre`, `magasinier`.

> **Consultation mode (read-only) at the tenant level.** If the company has no active Stripe subscription (cancelled or absent), the entire tenant switches to **consultation mode**: reads remain permitted, but **any** write request returns **403** (`erp_auth.py:526`). Since the AI assistant's five endpoints are POSTs, they also fall under this rule: **the AI assistant is not usable in consultation mode.** See the Configuration / Subscription module to restore access.

> **A tenant that has never opened the page has empty tables.** The 7 `location_*` tables are created **on first use** (on the fly). A new tenant sees counters at **0** and empty lists: this is normal, not an error.

### 1.7 Costs and billing (AI assistant only)

- **The entire register part is free**: managing the catalog, contracts, lines, returns, and labor lending consumes no credit.
- **Only the AI assistant is paid.** Each call to one of the five tools consumes the company's **prepaid AI credits**. The cost is the used model's rate **plus a 30% markup**:
  - **Claude Sonnet 4-6** tools (free-form question, checklist, rent vs. buy): US$3 per million input tokens, US$15 per million output tokens, × 1.30.
  - **Claude Opus 4-8** tools (equipment recommendation, contract analysis): US$5 per million input tokens, US$25 per million output tokens, × 1.30.
- The exact cost shows under each answer: "**Cost: US${amount}**". Usage is tracked under a `location_chat`, `location_recommander`, `location_analyser_contrat`, `location_checklist`, or `location_compare_achat` feature in the super administrator's AI usage tracking.
- An account with **no credits** gets a **402** "Insufficient AI credits" error and cannot run the tool; the rental register itself remains free.

> **Warning — no idempotency on the AI charge.** The credit charge is performed **with no idempotency key**: a **double-click** or a **network retry** on the same call can **charge twice**. Wait for the answer (or the error message) before re-running a tool.

### 2.5 Returns tab

Source: `:1101-1369`. This tab organizes **returns by inspection**.

**"Active contracts — awaiting return ({N})" card**: contracts whose status is `EN_COURS`, `RESERVE`, `ACTIF`, or `EN_RETARD`. For each contract: client name, number, and "Since {date}", a **"View lines"** button. **"Return inspection" window**:

| Field | Type | Note |
|-------|------|------|
| Checkout condition | read-only badge | reminder of the condition at checkout |
| Condition after return | menu (6 conditions) | the condition observed on return |
| Observed damage | text area | "Describe the damage observed..." |
| Repair fee ($) · Cleaning fee ($) · Late fee ($) | number · number · number | billable (see below) |
| Comments | text area | — |

> **Effects of a return.** The server locks the line, **refuses a double return** (409), deducts the equipment **from the line itself** (a value sent by the client would be ignored), sets the actual return date to now, records the condition after, **propagates that condition to the equipment record** (damaged material does not stay "Good") and, when **all** the contract's lines are returned, **moves the contract to `RETOURNE`** automatically.

> **Automatic late fees.** If you **leave the "Late fee" field empty** and the return is late, the server computes the delay itself by bringing the rate down to a daily equivalent (divisor 7 for a weekly rate, 30 for a monthly rate) × quantity × days late. A **FORFAIT is excluded** from this automatic calculation (otherwise the entire flat rate would be re-billed per day late). Entering an explicit 0 in "Late fee" means a waiver (distinct from leaving the field empty).

| Tool | Model | What you enter |

### 2.9 Cross-cutting elements

- **Search**: always **local** (it filters the already-loaded page). Present in Catalog and Rentals.
- **Sorting**: click the column headers of the main tables.
- **Status change**: via **dropdown** (in a contract's detail, or in a lending contract's row), applied immediately.
- **No on-screen pagination**: the catalog and the contracts are loaded up to **200 items** at once (the server-side maximum).
- **No export, printing, CSV, PDF, upload, or bulk action** anywhere.

## 3. Step-by-step workflows

### 3.1 Add equipment to the catalog

1. **Catalog** tab → **"Add an item"**.
2. Enter the **Name** (required); as needed the category, condition, make, model, year, **total quantity**, and purchase and replacement values.
3. Fill in the daily, weekly, and/or monthly **rates**, the **required deposit**, and the **"Insurance required"** flag.
4. (Optional) Check **"Tiered rate active"** and set the **Threshold (days)** and the **Reduction (%)** for an automatic discount on long durations.
5. **Create**. The equipment is immediately active and available; its availability is recalculated on every movement.

### 3.2 Create a rental contract

1. **Rentals** tab → **"New contract"**.
2. Enter the **Client** (required, free text); as needed the client type, dates, duration type, number of periods, delivery location, deposit, conditions, and notes.
3. **Create**. The server assigns the `LOC-#####` number and sets the status to `BROUILLON`. The contract appears in the table.

### 3.3 Add equipment (lines) to a contract

1. **Rentals** tab → click the contract's row to open its **detail**.
2. In the line form: choose the **Equipment**, the **Qty**, the **Rate ($)**, the **Type** (day / week / month / flat), the **Discount (%)**, and the **checkout and expected return dates**.
3. **Add**. The server computes the line amount (duration × rate × quantity, minus the discount, then the tiered rate if it applies) and **recomputes the totals** of the contract (pre-tax, GST, QST, total incl. tax).

> **Oversell guard.** If you ask for more units than are available (given the other active contracts), the server refuses the line with error **409 "Insufficient availability"**. Same check if you **increase** the quantity of an existing line.

### 3.4 Advance a contract (change the status)

1. Open the contract detail.
2. In the header, choose the new **Status** from the dropdown: `BROUILLON` → `RESERVE` → `EN_COURS` → `RETOURNE` → `FACTURE`, plus `ANNULE` and `EN_RETARD` as needed.
3. The change is applied immediately.

> **Activation guard.** The server **refuses (422)** to move a contract to `EN_COURS` or `FACTURE` if it contains **no line**. Add at least one piece of equipment first.

> **Reminder.** Moving a contract to `FACTURE` creates **no invoice** in accounting: it only changes the status.

### 3.5 Record a return with inspection

1. **Returns** tab → find the contract in "Active contracts — awaiting return" → **"View lines"**.
2. On a non-returned line, click **"Record return"**.
3. In the inspection window: choose the **Condition after return**, describe the **Observed damage**, enter the **charges** as needed (repair, cleaning, late), and comments.
4. **Record return**. The line is marked returned, the equipment's condition is updated, and if all the contract's lines are returned, the contract moves to `RETOURNE`.

> Leave "Late fee" **empty** for the system to compute the delay for you; enter **0** to explicitly waive late fees.

### 3.6 Delete a contract

1. Open the contract detail.
2. The **"Delete this contract"** button only appears if the status is `BROUILLON` or `ANNULE`. Otherwise, first move the contract to `ANNULE`.
3. Confirm. The contract, its lines, and its returns are deleted; the equipment's availability is recalculated.

### 3.7 Configure an employee for labor lending

1. **Labor** tab → **Employees** sub-tab.
2. If the employee is not in the list: **"Add an employee"**, pick them from the unconfigured HR employees.
3. Click **"Configure"** on their row: check **"Available for lending"**, set the **status**, the **primary trade**, the hourly and daily **rates**, add notes.
4. **Save**.

### 3.8 Create a labor-lending contract

1. **Labor** tab → **Contracts** sub-tab → **"New employee contract"**.
2. Choose the **Employee** (required, only the "available" ones), the **Start date** and the **Expected end date** (required); as needed the rate type, unit rate, planned hours, location, and assignment description.
3. **Create**. The server assigns the `EMP-#####` number and sets the status to `BROUILLON`.
4. Advance the contract via its row's status menu (`EN_COURS` sets the employee to "On loan"; `TERMINE` / `FACTURE` / `ANNULE` returns them to "Available").

### 3.9 Enter hours on a lending contract

1. **Labor** tab → **Hours** sub-tab.
2. Choose the **Employee contract** (only `EN_COURS` / `ACTIF`), the **Work date**, the **regular** and **overtime** hours, the task description.
3. **Record hours**. The hours are added to the contract's actual-hours counter.

> With the offered rate types (day, week, month, flat), entering hours generates **no** automatic billable amount; it serves for tracking. Post the billing in accounting manually.

### 3.10 Use the AI assistant

1. **AI Assistant** tab → choose the tool.
2. Fill in the fields (for example, for "Rent vs. buy": the equipment, the purchase price, the daily rental rate, and the annual usage).
3. **Run**. The answer appears (text or JSON); the **cost** shows below and is charged to the company's AI credits.

> Press **"Run"** only once and wait for the answer: the charge has no double-click protection (see 1.7).

### 3.11 Understanding a refusal (403 / consultation mode / 402)

- **403 on a creation or a deletion** while you are logged in: your role is probably not in the write list (administrator, manager, foreman, storekeeper). Ask an administrator.
- **All** writes fail for **everyone**, and the AI assistant returns 403: the tenant is in **consultation mode** (inactive Stripe subscription). Regularize the subscription to restore writing.
- **402 "Insufficient AI credits"** in the assistant: top up the company's AI credit balance (Configuration / Subscription module). The rental register itself remains free.

|------|-------|--------|

**Billing chain (identical for all five)**:

1. `check_ai_guard(user)` → **neutral in practice**: always returns "authorized" for an authenticated user (`ai.py:824-825`). Never blocks.
2. `_check_credits(user)` → **the real gatekeeper**: super administrator unlimited; internal instance (`BILLING_ENABLED=false`) unlimited; otherwise reads the month's prepaid credits, **automatically tops up** if needed (Stripe), **fails closed** on error, and returns **402 "Insufficient AI credits"** if the balance is exhausted.
3. Call to Claude (offloaded off the event loop; response capped at 32,000 tokens).
4. Usage tracking + **credit charge** — **with no idempotency key** (see the warning in 1.7).

**Cost**: the model's rate **plus a 30% markup** — Sonnet `(input × 0.003 + output × 0.015) ÷ 1000 × 1.30`; Opus `(input × 0.005 + output × 0.025) ÷ 1000 × 1.30`.

> **Anti-injection.** The free-text fields of the analyzed contract and the equipment name from "rent vs. buy" are **sanitized** before being sent to the model (whitespace is collapsed, length is capped at 2,000 characters), and the prompt reminds it never to interpret this data as instructions.

### 4.9 Calculations and formulas

- **Line amount**: `amount = unit rate × quantity × duration × (1 − discount/100)`. Then, if the equipment has an **active tiered rate** and the duration **in days** reaches the threshold: `amount × (1 − reduction/100)`.
- **Billed duration** by rate type:
  - `JOUR`: number of days between checkout and expected return.
  - `SEMAINE`: that day count divided by 7, **rounded up**.
  - `MOIS`: divided by 30, **rounded up**.
  - `FORFAIT`: **always 1** (no multiplier — a guardrail so a flat rate is not multiplied by 30 days).
  - Failing dates, it falls back to the **number of periods** entered.
- **Contract totals**: `pre-tax = sum of line amounts + sum of return charges (repair + cleaning + late)`. Then `GST = pre-tax × 0.05`, `QST = pre-tax × 0.09975`, `total incl. tax = pre-tax + GST + QST` (rounded to 2 decimals).

  > **Return charges are billable and taxed.** They flow into the pre-tax amount, thus into the taxes and the total — otherwise the damage billed to the client would vanish from the amount.

  > **Tax rates hard-coded.** GST 5% and QST 9.975% are hard-coded (the rate column in the database is `numeric(5,2)` in production and would round 9.975 to 9.98). There is **no** per-contract rate and no handling of a jurisdiction outside Quebec (United States, exempt): this is a backlog item.

- **Automatic late fees**: if "Late fee" is left empty and the return is late, the rate is brought down to a daily equivalent (divisor 7 for the week, 30 for the month) × quantity × days late; a `FORFAIT` is excluded from it. The value **0** entered explicitly counts as a waiver.
- **Availability**: `available quantity = max(0, total quantity − units out)`, where the **units out** are the sum of the quantities of the non-returned lines belonging to "immobilizing" contracts (`BROUILLON`, `RESERVE`, `EN_COURS`, `ACTIF`, `EN_RETARD`). Equipment with an empty total quantity is not tracked by quantity (it remains available as long as it is active). Availability is **recalculated** on every movement (line, return, status change, deletion).
- **Lending-contract amount**: computed **only** for an "hourly" rate (`estimated amount = rate × planned hours`; `billed amount = actual hours × rate`). Since the screen's menu does not offer this type, these amounts stay empty in normal use.

### 4.10 Validations, bounds, and errors

| Rule | Effect |
|------|--------|
| Empty equipment name | **422** (button also disabled on the screen) |
| Negative rate, quantity, or amount | **422** (`ge=0`) |
| Discount or tiered reduction outside 0-100 | **422** |
| Year of manufacture outside 1900-2100 | **422** |
| Tiered threshold outside 1-3,650 days | **422** |
| End date earlier than the start date (contract or loan) | **422** |
| Expected return date earlier than the checkout date (line) | **422** |
| Date field sent empty (`""`) | **422** (converted to no value) |
| Move to `EN_COURS`/`FACTURE` with no line at all | **422** |
| Checkout or quantity increase exceeding stock | **409 "Insufficient availability"** |
| Double return on the same line | **409** |
| Deletion of equipment engaged in an active contract | **400** |

> **Defense against SQL injection.** The dynamic updates (equipment, contract, line, lending contract) go through **column whitelists**; no field value builds SQL.

### 4.11 Automatic numbers

- **Rental contracts**: `LOC-` followed by 5 digits (the internal id, zero-padded) — example `LOC-00021`.
- **Lending contracts**: `EMP-` followed by 5 digits — example `EMP-00007`.
- **Atomic** generation: the server inserts a unique temporary value then replaces it with the final number in the same transaction (no collision, never `COUNT(*)+1`).

## 5. Integrations and FAQ

### 5.1 CRM, Companies, and Contacts (Modules 04, 05, 06)

> **Limited integration.** The rental contract keeps only a **free-text client name**. The columns `client_company_id` and `client_contact_id` exist in the database but are **not** entered by the screen. To link a contract to a CRM company or contact, you would have to go through the API directly. Consequence: the top-10 clients (Statistics) groups by **entered name**, sensitive to typos.

### 5.2 Projects (Module 09)

The contract carries a `project_id` in the database, **not** entered by the screen. No automatic link to a project; deleting a project does not touch rental contracts.

### 5.3 Accounting and Invoices (Module 15)

> **No invoice, no accounting entry.** The `FACTURE` status only **changes the status**: the amounts (pre-tax, GST, QST, total incl. tax) stay in the contract table. Post them manually in the Accounting module. The deposit does not enter the contract total and generates no entry.

### 5.4 Store / Inventory (Module 10)

> **Separate modules.** Rental equipment (`location_items`) is **not** linked to Store products. The same material present in both must be entered on both sides. Renting equipment **does not change** the Store stock.

### 5.5 Employees / HR, Time tracking, and Payroll (Modules 11, 13)

- Labor lending **reads** the HR-module employees (for the name and the trade) via the rental configuration table.
- The **hours** entered in lending **do not go** into Payroll or Time tracking: it is a parallel bookkeeping, specific to lending.
- To bill a loan, post the amount manually (the automatic calculation exists only for an "hourly" rate not offered on the screen, see 4.9).

### 5.6 Logistics (Module 20) and Maintenance (Module 22) — scope overlap

- **Logistics → Equipment** declares the equipment **owned or rented by the company** for **internal** use (site fleet), with its maintenance.
- **This module (Rental)** manages the **rental contracts offered to clients**, with lines, taxes, deposits, and returns. **Separate** tables and logic.
- **Maintenance (Module 22)** covers equipment servicing. The `REPARATION` condition of a rental piece of equipment is a mere flag: **no automatic link** to a maintenance order. Manual tracking (move the equipment to `REPARATION`, then restore its condition after repair).

### 5.7 AI Credits (Module 25)

- The rental assistant shares the **same AI credit wallet** as the ERP's other assistants.
- Each call is tracked under the features `location_chat`, `location_recommander`, `location_analyser_contrat`, `location_checklist`, or `location_compare_achat`, visible in the super administrator's usage tracking.

### 5.8 Frequently asked questions

**Can I print a rental contract or a release slip?**
No. The module has **no** export or printing: no PDF, no CSV, no printable return slip. It produces no document handed to the client.

**Does the module invoice automatically at the end of a rental?**
No. The `FACTURE` status only changes the status. No invoice is created in accounting; posting is manual.

**How do I know whether a piece of equipment is free over a future period?**
The module does not manage a reservation calendar. It knows an **available quantity** (total minus units out) and only refuses to exceed the physical stock at checkout time. For a future period, check the Returns tab (non-returned lines) and manage the overlap manually.

**Why can't I change the equipment of an existing line?**
The Equipment field is locked when editing. Delete the line and recreate it with the right equipment.

**Why does a lending contract's "Amount" column stay "--"?**
Because the automatic calculation exists only for an "hourly" rate that the menu does not offer (it offers day, week, month, flat). With these types, no amount is produced; enter it via the API if needed, or post it in accounting.

**Does the tiered rate really work?**
Yes. This is a correction versus the old manual: the "Tiered rate active" checkbox (plus the threshold and the reduction) is exposed on the screen **and** the discount is applied to the calculation as soon as the duration in days reaches the threshold.

**How do I handle a late return?**
Move the contract to `EN_RETARD` if needed, then, on return, leave "Late fee" empty for an automatic delay calculation, or enter an amount. Enter 0 to waive the fee.

**What becomes of the damage charges entered at inspection?**
They are **billable**: they add to the contract's pre-tax subtotal (thus taxed) and flow into the total. They are not lost.

**Can an employee be on two lending contracts at the same time?**
Technically yes, but their "On loan" status is overwritten by the last contract moved to `EN_COURS`. To be managed manually.

**A brand-new tenant shows counters at zero: is that normal?**
Yes. The 7 `location_*` tables are created on the first entry. As soon as your first record or first contract is entered, the lists fill up.

**Who can create and delete records?**
The administrator, super administrator, manager, foreman, and storekeeper roles (or any `is_admin` account). Other roles get a 403 error. Reading is open to all.

**Can the AI assistant modify my data?**
No. It **advises** only (recommendations, comparisons, checklists, analysis). It writes nothing. Validate its results — especially costs and safety — with a professional.

**Why does the AI assistant return 403 even though I have credits?**
Probably consultation mode: since its calls are writes (POST), they are blocked when the Stripe subscription is inactive. Regularize the subscription.

**How much does a call to the assistant cost?**
The model's rate (Sonnet or Opus depending on the tool) plus a 30% markup, charged to your AI credits. The exact cost shows under each answer. The rental register itself is free.

- **Purpose**: an internal, two-part operational register — **equipment rental** to clients (catalog, taxed contracts, returns by inspection) and **labor lending** (lent employees) — plus an **advisory AI assistant**.
- **Access**: sidebar → **FIELD** group → **Rental** (`HardHat` icon), route `/location`, title "Equipment Rental". Default tab: Dashboard.

- **Permissions**: reading open to all; writing reserved for the administrator / super administrator / manager / foreman / storekeeper roles (or `is_admin`); consultation mode (read-only, including the AI assistant) if the Stripe subscription is inactive.
