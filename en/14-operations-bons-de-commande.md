# Module 14 — Purchase Orders (supplier purchasing)

> **Version**: 3.0 (complete overhaul verified against the actual source code)
> **Reference code**:
> - Frontend: `frontend/src/pages/MagasinPage.tsx` (≈ 3,425 lines — the single Store page; purchase orders live in the "Orders" tab), `frontend/src/components/rfq/RfqTab.tsx` (≈ 1,412 lines — bridge from Requests for Quote to purchase orders), API `frontend/src/api/suppliers.ts`, `frontend/src/api/rfq.ts`, `frontend/src/api/accounting.ts`
> - Backend: `backend/routers/suppliers.py` (≈ 2,935 lines — suppliers + purchase orders, 15 endpoints dedicated to POs), `backend/routers/rfq.py` (≈ 2,434 lines — awarding a request for quote, which generates the POs), `backend/routers/accounting.py` (supplier invoicing + automatic posting to the general ledger), `backend/routers/inventory.py` (reversing movement when a receipt is cancelled)
> - API prefix: `/api/erp/v1`
> **PostgreSQL tables (per tenant)**: `bons_commande` (PO header), `bon_commande_lignes` (line items — table **shared** with requests for quote), `mouvements_stock` and `produits` (costed receiving), `dossier_achats` (attachment to the opportunity case file), `fournisseurs` / `companies` (supplier name); table shared between tenants: `public.bc_public_tokens` (public-link tokens).
> **Scope**: a **purchase order** (PO) is the purchase-commitment document the company sends to a supplier. This module covers the entire **purchasing cycle**: **create** a PO (by hand, by **AI scan** of an invoice or a bid, or by **awarding** a request for quote), **complete** it (line items, delivery, notes), **send** it to the supplier (email + public link), **track** it by status, **receive** it — which **automatically replenishes stock** and computes a receiving cost — then **invoice** it (draft supplier invoice). **Important vocabulary point**: there is **no "Purchase Orders" menu entry**. POs are the **"Orders" tab** of the **Store** menu (`/magasin`), which is the tab open by default. This manual is the detailed companion to **Module 10 — Store** (which presents the seven tabs as a whole): it focuses on purchase orders and on the bridge from requests for quote.

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Module mission

The Purchase Orders module is the company's **purchasing desk**. It lets you:

- **create** a numbered purchase order (`BC-00012`) in a supplier's name, optionally attached to a project and a responsible employee;
- **fill in** the PO: line items (from the product catalog or entered by hand), delivery address and terms, internal notes;
- speed up entry with an **AI scan**: upload the image or PDF of a supplier's purchase order, invoice or bid, and let the artificial intelligence extract the line items;
- **transmit** the PO to the supplier by **email**, with a **public link** viewable without an account for 90 days;
- **track** progress through a color-coded status (Draft, Sent, Confirmed, In progress, Received, Invoiced, Cancelled);
- **receive** the goods: moving the PO to **Received** **automatically increases the stock** of the linked products and records a costed inbound movement, atomically and with double-counting protection;
- **export** the PO as printable HTML, as Excel (.xlsx), or as a QuickBooks-compatible CSV;
- **invoice** the PO: create a **draft supplier invoice** in Accounting, which freezes the PO at the Invoiced status;
- retrieve the POs **generated by awarding** a request for quote (a call for bids to several suppliers).

### 1.2 What the module does NOT do

- **There is no "Purchase Orders" menu.** The sidebar shows **Store** (cart icon, Operations section). Purchase orders are the **"Orders" tab** of that page. This is an important correction versus older versions of this manual.
- **No partial receiving.** A PO is received **in full**, in a single operation. There is no "received quantity" per line, nor multiple or staggered receipts.
- **Receiving is irreversible.** Once the PO has moved to **Received**, it cannot be brought back to another status (the system returns an error to protect inventory). A mistaken receipt is corrected by **cancelling the generated stock movements** (Movements tab), not by un-validating the PO.
- **Lines lock after receiving or invoicing.** You can no longer add or remove a line on a **Received** or **Invoiced** PO.
- **Deletion is blocked** for a PO that is **Received**, **Invoiced**, or already **posted** to the ledger.
- **No workflow buttons** ("Confirm", "Mark In progress"…). The status is changed **by hand** in a dropdown menu. Only **sending by email** automatically advances the status (and only from Draft to Sent).
- **The product's cost price is not recalculated on receipt.** Receiving computes a receiving cost (weighted average of the PO's lines) and records it on the **stock movement**; it **does not overwrite** the cost price entered on the product record. There is **no moving weighted average cost**.
- **At most two taxes** (for example GST and QST), **frozen** at PO creation.
- **The AI scan writes nothing.** It extracts the lines into the creation window; you must review, correct, then create the PO yourself.

### 1.3 Where to find purchase orders (access)

- **Sidebar** → **Operations** section → **Store** (cart icon).
- **Address**: `/magasin`.
- **Default tab**: **Orders** (it is the first tab; it opens automatically when you land on the page).
- **Opening a PO directly**: a link of the form `/magasin?open=<id>` (for example from the 360 view of a case file) directly opens the relevant purchase order.
- Protected page: you must be authenticated within a tenant.

> **Vocabulary reminder.** Look for "Store" in the menu, then the "Orders" tab. There is no "Purchase Orders" or "Purchasing" entry in the sidebar. The supplier-record tab is called **"Supplier"** (singular).

### 1.4 Permissions and roles

**Viewing** purchase orders is open to any authenticated user of the tenant. **Writing** depends on the role, and two distinct guards intersect in this module:

| Guard | What it protects | Authorized roles |
|-------|--------------------|-----------------|
| **Purchasing write** (`require_purchase_write`) | Create / edit / delete a purchase order, add / remove lines, change dates and status, send by email, scan a PO, create / edit a supplier | administrator (`is_admin`), super-admin, **admin**, **manager**, **storekeeper** or **user** |
| **RFQ write** (`require_rfq_write`) | Create / manage a request for quote, invite suppliers, enter prices and **award** (which generates the POs) | administrator (`is_admin`), super-admin, admin, manager, **storekeeper** |

> **Key nuance.** The **user** role can create and receive purchase orders, but **cannot award a request for quote**: awarding requires a **manager** or **storekeeper** role (or administrator). Conversely, the **employee** and **accountant** roles **cannot** write in purchasing (read-only). The `require_purchase_write` guard tests the user's **actual role** (not the token type), so as to effectively refuse "employee" and "accountant" while admitting any administrator by default.
>
> **Invoicing.** Creating a supplier invoice from a PO (the "Create invoice" button) relies on Accounting and requires only an authenticated user; read-only mode and the other access protections apply upstream.

### 1.5 Statistics cards (KPIs)

Four figure cards permanently sit atop the Store page, **above** the tab bar. They concern **inventory as a whole**, not purchase orders: **Products** (number of active products), **Low-stock alert** (products below their threshold), **Stock value** (in dollars) and **Categories**. These cards are described in detail in **Module 10 — Store**. There is **no** KPI card specific to purchase orders.

### 1.6 Key concepts

- **Purchase order (PO)**: the purchase commitment with a supplier. Technically, a row in the `bons_commande` table, numbered `BC-00012` (five digits). It carries a supplier, a project, a responsible employee, dates, a status, a delivery address and terms, two frozen taxes, totals and notes.
- **Line item**: a material or service ordered. Each line carries a description, a quantity, a unit, a unit price and an amount (quantity × price). A line can be **tied to a catalog product** (which enables receiving into stock) or remain **free** (off-inventory).
- **Status**: the PO's state in its lifecycle. Seven values (see §1.7). Moving to **Received** is the only one with a material effect (on stock).
- **Receiving**: the action of moving the PO to **Received**. It **increases the stock** of the linked products and creates a costed **inbound movement** per product. It is **atomic** (all or nothing) and **final**.
- **Receiving cost**: for a received product, the unit cost recorded on the movement = **total amount of that product's lines ÷ total quantity** (weighted average of that **PO's** lines). This cost serves audit and accounting; it **does not update** the cost price on the product record.
- **Public link**: a token that lets the supplier view the PO **without an account**, for 90 days. Created when sent by email.
- **Awarding**: the action, within a **request for quote** (RFQ), of selecting suppliers, which **generates one purchase order per selected supplier** (see §2.9 and §3.12).
- **Tax snapshot**: the PO's two tax rates are **frozen at its creation** based on the tenant's configuration (Quebec by default: GST 5%, QST 9.975%). Changing the company configuration later does not affect POs already created.

### 1.7 Purchase order lifecycle

Seven statuses are recognized by the system (`VALID_BC_STATUTS`, `suppliers.py:1206`):

| Status | Badge color | Meaning |
|--------|------------------|---------------|
| **Draft** | gray | Created, not yet transmitted |
| **Sent** | indigo | Transmitted to the supplier (by email) |
| **Confirmed** | blue | The supplier has confirmed the order |
| **In progress** | purple | Delivery underway |
| **Received** | green | Goods received → **stock has been replenished** |
| **Invoiced** | teal | A supplier invoice was created from the PO |
| **Cancelled** | red | Order cancelled |

> **Statuses are "free" except for two exceptions.** There is **no** strict state machine for purchase orders: you choose the status by hand in the dropdown of the detail panel. The **Confirmed** and **In progress** statuses are valid and displayed, but **no automatic action** triggers them — they are set manually, for your own tracking. **Two transitions are automatic**: (1) **sending by email** moves a PO from **Draft** to **Sent** (and only in that direction); (2) **creating an invoice** moves the PO to **Invoiced**. Moving to **Received**, for its part, triggers receiving into stock — and becomes final.

Two buttons and a search box sit atop the list:

- **Supplier order** (main button, "+" icon): opens the purchase-order creation window (see §2.6). This same button is also present in the **Supplier** tab's bar.
- **Scan a PO (AI)** (scan icon): opens a file picker (image or PDF) for AI-assisted extraction. During processing, the button shows **"Analysis in progress…"** (see §2.7).
- **Search**: filters the list by number, supplier or project.

### 2.3 Purchase order table

Columns (sortable and resizable):

| Column | Content | Detail |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| **Number** | `BC-00012` | Shown in link color |
| **Supplier** | Supplier name | "--" if empty |
| **Project** | Associated project | "--" if empty |

**Behaviors**:

Leaving the field **empty clears** the date. Anti-race protection prevents a late response from overwriting a more recent entry.

> **The list's "Amount" is a before-tax amount.** The column shows the subtotal (`montant_total`), i.e. the sum of the lines **before** taxes. The PO detail, for its part, shows the subtotal, the taxes and the tax-included total (see §2.5).

### 2.4 The seven statuses and their colors

See the table in §1.7. The status badge appears in the list and in the detail panel. If a PO carries an unexpected status (for example inherited from an older version), it is displayed in gray and remains editable.

### 2.5 Purchase order detail panel

Selecting a PO opens a panel on the right. Its sections, top to bottom:

**Header**
- PO number (monospace font) and supplier name (defaults to "Supplier" if the name is missing).
- Colored **status dropdown**: the seven standard statuses. If the PO carries a value outside the list, it is added to the menu so it is not lost. Changing the status marks the PO as "modified" (you will need to **Save**).
- **Close** button (X). On a phone, a "Back to purchase orders" button.

**"Information" section**
- **Order date** (date field).
- **Expected delivery date** (date field).
- **Responsible employee**: menu of **active** employees ("None" option).
- **Associated project**: menu of projects ("None" option).

**"Delivery" section**
- **Delivery address** (text area; example "123 Sample St., Granby, QC J2G 8R5").
- **Delivery terms** (text area; "Lead times, site access, on-site contact…").

**"Items ({count})" section**
- **"Select inventory item"** menu: choosing a catalog product **automatically fills in** the description, unit and price.
- **Description**, **Qty**, **Unit price ($)** fields.
- **"Add line"** button: adds the item. Disabled as long as no description or product is entered. The server requires a **quantity greater than zero** and a **product or a description**.
- Each existing line shows "description" then "quantity unit × price = **amount**", with a button to remove it.
- Empty: "No lines. Add items above."

**"Totals" section** (if the PO has lines)
- **Subtotal (before tax)**, **tax 1** (GST 5% by default), **tax 2** (QST 9.975% by default), **Total (tax included)**. The taxes come from the configuration **frozen on the PO**; a tax line is hidden if its rate is zero.

**"Internal notes" section** (text area)

**Actions — first row**

| Button | Effect |
|--------|-------|
| **Generate HTML** | Downloads the PO as a printable `.html` file (`{number}.html`) |
| **Preview** | Opens the document in an embedded window (see §2.8) |
| **Send to supplier** | Opens the email sending window (see §2.8) |
| **QuickBooks CSV** | Downloads a QuickBooks-compatible CSV file (`{number}_quickbooks.csv`), generated **in the browser** |
| **Copy CSV** | Copies that same CSV to the clipboard |
| **Excel (.xlsx)** | Downloads the PO as a spreadsheet |
| **Create invoice** | Creates a (draft) supplier invoice from the PO. **Disabled** if the PO is already **Invoiced** (tooltip "Already invoiced") |

**Actions — second row**
- **Delete** (danger): deletes the PO (same rules as §3.11).
- **Save**: saves the changes. **Disabled** as long as no field has changed (tooltip "No changes to save").

> **Targeted save (overwrite protection).** The **Save** button sends the server **only the fields that actually changed**. That is what prevents you, when reopening an old tab, from accidentally overwriting the status of a PO that a colleague just moved to "Received" (which, otherwise, could have rolled it back to "Draft" and silently undone the receipt). It is also through this **Save** button that **receiving** happens: choose "Received" in the status menu, then **Save**.

### 2.6 The "Create a new purchase order" window

Opened by **Supplier order**. Its sections:

- **AI extraction banner** (only after a scan): "{n} line(s) extracted by the AI", the **confidence** level (high / medium / low), the extracted total, and a warning if the supplier was not recognized ("Check and correct the data before creating the purchase order.").
- **Supplier** (required): **"Select a supplier *"** menu. Without a supplier, creation is blocked.
- **Assignment**: **Associated project** ("No project"), **Responsible employee** ("None (default: the logged-in user)"), **Expected delivery date** ("Optional. Visible on the PDF sent to the supplier.").
- **Items**: one line per item. Choose a **product** from the catalog **or** "Manual entry (off-inventory)", then **Qty**, **Unit price $** and the **Amount** (calculated). In manual entry, two extra fields: **Description** and **Unit** ("EA, m, ft, h…"). **"Add an item"** button. A **live tax summary** shows the subtotal, GST, QST and total.
- **Delivery (optional)**: address and terms.
- **Notes (optional)**: internal notes ("Notes visible internally, not printed on the purchase order.").
- Footer: **Cancel** / **Create the purchase order** (disabled without a supplier).

> **How creation actually works.** The server first creates the PO **header** with its lines (Draft status, number `BC-00012`). It then saves, in a second call, the "advanced" fields (responsible employee, delivery address and terms). If that second call fails, the PO still exists and a message warns you that "some advanced fields were not saved" (to be completed from the detail panel). Likewise, if a line could not be added, a message states "X/Y line(s) not added".

### 2.7 AI scan of a purchase order (Vision)

The **Scan a PO (AI)** button (Orders tab) lets you start from an existing document instead of typing everything:

1. Choose an **image** (PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, BMP) or a **PDF**, up to **20 MB**.
2. The artificial intelligence (Vision model) reads the document and **pre-fills** the creation window: detected supplier, dates, line items, notes.
3. You **review and correct**, choose the supplier if the AI did not recognize it, then create the PO through the normal flow.

> **The scan creates nothing on its own.** It is a **read-only** operation: no data is written until you click **Create the purchase order**. The scan **consumes** prepaid **AI credits** (see §4.8). If credits are exhausted or the AI is not configured, the scan is refused.

### 2.8 Sending to the supplier and HTML preview

**"Send the purchase order" window** (**Send to supplier** button):
- Enter the **supplier's email**, then **Send**.
- The system generates a **shareable public link valid for 90 days** (copyable via "Copy link"), which shows the PO read-only without authentication.
- The email, themed to the company's colors, is sent to the supplier.
- The status moves to **Sent** **only if the PO was in Draft**; a PO already further along keeps its status.

**HTML preview window** (**Preview** button):
- Displays the document in an embedded window (sandboxed frame).
- **"Open in a new tab"** button (protected against tab hijacking) and **"Close"**.

### 2.9 The "Requests for Quote → purchase order" bridge (awarding)

Purchase orders can also **originate from a request for quote** (RFQ), i.e. from a call for bids to several suppliers. This cycle lives in the neighboring **"Requests for Quote"** tab (described in **Module 10**). The junction point is **awarding**:

- After comparing the offers, you **check the selected suppliers**, then click **"Award → generate PO"** (confirmation "Award to the selected suppliers and generate the purchase orders?").
- The system creates **one purchase order per selected supplier**, at **Draft** status, with the note "Issu de la demande de prix {number}" ("From request for quote {number}").
- Only the **actually priced lines** (price greater than zero) are carried over; if a response has no priced line, awarding that supplier is refused (never a $0 PO).
- The RFQ's delivery address and terms are copied onto the POs.
- A message confirms: "Award completed. Purchase order(s): {numbers}". The POs then appear in the **Orders** tab, ready to be completed and sent.

> POs from an award behave **exactly** like POs created by hand: same statuses, same receiving, same invoicing.

## 3. Step-by-step processes

### 3.1 Create a purchase order by hand

1. **Orders** tab → **Supplier order** (or **Supplier** tab → **Supplier order**).
2. Choose the **supplier** (required).
3. Fill in as needed the **project**, the **responsible employee** and the **expected delivery date**.
4. Add the **items**: choose a catalog product (description, unit and price fill in) or enter a line by hand (description + unit). Taxes are computed live.
5. Fill in as needed the **address** and **delivery terms**, and some **internal notes**.
6. **Create the purchase order**. It appears at **Draft** status, numbered `BC-00012`.

> If a message reports "advanced fields not saved" or "line(s) not added", open the PO and complete it from the detail panel.

### 3.2 Create a PO by AI scan

1. **Orders** tab → **Scan a PO (AI)**.
2. Choose an **image** or a **PDF** (up to 20 MB). The button shows "Analysis in progress…".
3. When finished, the creation window opens pre-filled, with a banner (line count, confidence, total).
4. **Review and correct** the lines; choose the supplier if not recognized.
5. **Create the purchase order**.

> The scan consumes AI credits. It creates nothing until you confirm.

### 3.3 Add, edit or remove lines

1. Open the PO (click the row) → **Items** section.
2. **Add**: choose an inventory item (auto-fill) or enter the description, quantity and price, then **Add line**.
3. **Remove** a line: the "Remove line" button on the relevant line.
4. The subtotal, taxes and total recalculate automatically.

> **Lines lock after receiving or invoicing.** The server **refuses** to add or remove a line on a **Received** or **Invoiced** PO ("Cannot modify the lines of a received or invoiced purchase order"). Correct the lines **before** receiving the PO.

### 3.4 Edit a PO's dates

- **From the list**: click the **Order Date** or **Expected Delivery** cell, choose the date, then leave the field. Leaving the field empty clears the date.
- **From the detail**: edit the date fields in the **Information** section, then **Save**.

### 3.5 Change a PO's status

1. Open the PO → **status** dropdown in the panel header.
2. Choose the new status (Draft, Sent, Confirmed, In progress, Received, Invoiced, Cancelled).
3. **Save**.

> **Watch out for "Received".** Choosing "Received" then Saving **triggers receiving into stock** (see §3.7) and becomes **final**. The **Confirmed** and **In progress** statuses are purely informational (no automatic effect). The **Invoiced** status is normally set by creating an invoice (§3.10), not by hand.

### 3.6 Send a PO to the supplier

1. Open the PO → **Send to supplier**.
2. Enter the supplier's **email** address, then send.
3. The system creates a **public link** (90 days), sends the email and, if the PO was in **Draft**, moves it to **Sent**. Copy the link as needed to share it another way.

### 3.7 Receive a PO (replenish stock)

1. Open the PO → in the header, choose the **Received** status → **Save**.
2. The system, **automatically and atomically**:
   - groups the lines **by product** (so as not to count twice);
   - **increases the stock** of each linked product by the received quantity;
   - creates an **inbound movement** per product, with the reference "Réception BC {number}" and a **unit cost** = weighted average of that product's lines in the PO.
3. The PO now shows **Received**, and the movements appear in the **Movements** tab.

> **Two guarantees.** Receiving is **serialized**: if two people receive the same PO at the same time, stock is **never** doubled. And it is **all or nothing**: if recording the movement fails, stock is not increased (no possible stock / audit divergence). A cap protects against an aberrant cost (beyond about 10 billion, receiving is refused with a clear message rather than a technical error).
>
> **The product's cost price does not change.** Receiving records a cost **on the movement** (for audit and accounting) and updates the **quantity** in stock, but it **does not overwrite** the cost price entered on the product record. To keep a cost price up to date, correct it by hand on the record (Module 10).

### 3.8 Correct a receipt made in error

A **Received** PO cannot be rolled back: if you try to move it to another status, the system returns an error ("Receiving a purchase order is final…"). To rectify:

1. **Movements** tab → find the inbound movements "Réception BC {number}".
2. Open each movement → **Cancel this movement**.
3. The system creates an outbound **reversing movement** that brings stock back to its prior level.

> The PO **stays** at "Received" status (for audit); it is **inventory** that you correct, through the reversing movements. See Module 10 for cancelling movements.

### 3.9 Export a PO (HTML, Excel, QuickBooks CSV)

From the detail panel, first row of actions:

- **Generate HTML**: downloads a printable `.html` file (open it, then Ctrl+P to print or save as a PDF).
- **Preview**: shows the same document in an embedded window, with "Open in a new tab".
- **Excel (.xlsx)**: downloads the PO as a spreadsheet.
- **QuickBooks CSV**: downloads a QuickBooks-compatible CSV (generated in your browser). **Copy CSV** puts that same content on the clipboard.

### 3.10 Create a supplier invoice from a PO

1. Open the PO → **Create invoice** → confirmation.
2. A **draft supplier invoice** is created in Accounting, carrying over the PO's lines and taxes.
3. The PO moves to **Invoiced** status, and the "Create invoice" button becomes inactive ("Already invoiced").

> The server refuses to invoice the same PO twice (PO already invoiced or already posted), and refuses a PO with no line or no supplier. The invoice created stays **as a draft**: it is completed and posted in **Module 15 — Accounting**.

### 3.11 Delete a purchase order

1. From the list (trash on the row) **or** from the detail (**Delete**) → confirmation "Delete this purchase order?".
2. The PO and its lines are erased; the expenses and stock movements that referenced it are **detached** (they survive, without the link).

> **Deletion is refused** if the PO is **Received**, **Invoiced**, or already **posted** (a general-ledger entry is linked to it). In that last case, you must first **reverse the accounting entry** (Module 15). In other words, a PO that has affected stock or accounting cannot be deleted with a click — that is by design, to preserve traceability.

### 3.12 Award a request for quote (generates POs)

1. **Requests for Quote** tab → open the RFQ → gather the offers (see Module 10).
2. **View comparison** → check **Select** for the winning supplier(s).
3. **Award → generate PO** → confirmation.
4. The system creates **one purchase order (Draft) per selected supplier**, with the priced lines only, and displays the generated numbers.
5. Find these POs in the **Orders** tab to complete, send and receive them normally.

## 4. Reference

### 4.1 Endpoints (API)

All prefixed with `/api/erp/v1`. "Purchasing write" = the `require_purchase_write` guard (admin, super-admin, manager, storekeeper, user, or administrator).

**Purchase orders** (`suppliers.py`)

| Method + path | Role | Right |
|---|---|---|
| GET `/suppliers/purchase-orders` | Global list (pagination, status / project filters) | read |
| GET `/suppliers/{id}/orders` | A supplier's POs | read |
| POST `/suppliers/{id}/orders` | Create a PO | purchasing write |
| POST `/suppliers/orders/ai/scan` | Scan a PO (Vision AI, read-only) | purchasing write |
| PUT `/suppliers/purchase-orders/{id}` | Edit (dates / status / employee / project / address / terms / notes / taxes); **triggers receiving if the status moves to Received** | purchasing write |
| PUT `/suppliers/purchase-orders/{id}/dates` | Edit the dates (inline editing, drag in the Gantt) | purchasing write |
| PUT `/suppliers/purchase-orders/{id}/status` | Edit the status (same receiving effect as the unified PUT) | purchasing write |
| GET `/suppliers/orders/{id}/lines` | List the lines | read |

| Status | Color | Set by |

### 4.3 Numbering

Format `BC-00012` (`BC-` prefix + a five-digit identifier). The number is assigned in a **concurrency-safe** way: the PO is inserted with a temporary number, then renumbered from its identifier. Never `MAX + 1`. No year prefix (global sequential numbering).

### 4.4 Calculations

| Element | Formula | Triggered by |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| **Line amount** | `round(quantity × unit price, 2)` | Adding a line |
| **Subtotal (before tax)** | `Σ of line amounts` | Adding / removing a line, awarding |
| **Tax 1 (GST)** | `round(subtotal × rate 1 ÷ 100, 2)` | Totals recalculation |
| **Tax 2 (QST)** | `round(subtotal × rate 2 ÷ 100, 2)` | Totals recalculation |
| **Total (tax included)** | `subtotal + tax 1 + tax 2` | Totals recalculation |
| **Persisted `montant_total`** | `= subtotal (before tax)` | Totals recalculation |
| **Cost of a receipt (per product)** | `total amount of the product's lines ÷ total quantity` (weighted average of that **PO's** lines) | Move to Received |

> **Note on cost.** The "weighted average" aggregates only the lines of the **same purchase order** for the **same product**. It feeds the **receiving movement cost** (audit and accounting) but **does not recalculate** the product's cost price as a moving average. **There is no moving weighted average cost** in the system: a product's cost price remains the value entered by hand on its record (Module 10).

### 4.5 Taxes

The two taxes of a PO are **frozen at creation** based on the tenant's configuration (multi-province Canada or United States). Default values in Quebec: **GST 5%**, **QST 9.975%**. The rates are capped between 0 and 100, and the labels to 50 characters. A **legitimately empty** tax label (exemption) is preserved. Editing a rate in the detail panel **recalculates** the persisted totals. The HTML rendering, the Excel export and the email recalculate the taxes at display time (falling back to Quebec's default values for POs predating this feature).

### 4.6 Costed receiving (effect on stock)

Moving to **Received** (via the unified PUT or via the `/status` endpoint) executes, under a lock and atomically:

| Step | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Lock | `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` on the PO → **serializes** two concurrent receipts (anti double-stocking) |
| Aggregation | The lines are grouped **by product** (non-null `produit_id`) |
| Cost | Unit cost = total amount ÷ total quantity of the product in this PO |
| Stock | `stock_disponible += quantity` (under a lock on the product) |
| Movement | One **In** movement per product: reference "Réception BC {number}", type "BON_RECEPTION", unit cost and total cost, link to the PO |
| Safeguard | Total cost greater than ≈ 10 billion → refused (clear message, not a technical error) |
| Atomicity | Stock **and** movement, all or nothing; if one fails, nothing is applied |
| Idempotence | Saving "Received" again on an already-received PO **does not restock a second time** |

Only lines **tied to a product** feed the stock; free (off-inventory) lines have no material effect.

### 4.7 Locking rules

| Rule | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| Un-validate an already-**Received** PO (moving it back to another status) | Refused (HTTP 409) — cancel the stock movements instead |
| Add / remove a line on a **Received** or **Invoiced** PO | Refused (HTTP 400) |
| Delete a **Received** or **Invoiced** PO | Refused (HTTP 400) |
| Delete an already-**posted** PO (a linked general-ledger entry) | Refused (HTTP 400) — reverse the entry first |
| Create a PO with no supplier | Refused (supplier required) |
| Line with no positive quantity, or with no product and no description | Refused |
| Award a request for quote with no priced line at all | Refused (no $0 PO) |
| Scan file greater than 20 MB | Refused |
| Out-of-bounds amounts (numeric overflow) | Cleanly refused (error message) |

### 4.8 Money effect

Two effects, both important to know:

**1. Automatic posting to the general ledger.** An **accounting sync** (triggered from Accounting) automatically records a **purchase entry** for any PO whose status is **neither Cancelled nor Draft** (so Sent, Confirmed, In progress, Received or Invoiced), whose total is greater than zero, and which does not yet have an entry. The entry debits the expense account and the recoverable taxes (GST / QST), and credits accounts payable for the tax-included total. **Practical consequence**: as soon as a PO is **transmitted** (beyond draft) with an amount, it can be **posted at the next sync** — even if you have not yet created an invoice. This is a money effect that is not visible from the Orders tab alone.

**2. Prepaid AI credits.** The **AI scan** of a PO consumes the tenant's AI credits. The billed cost is the **actual token cost** (about $0.003 per thousand input tokens, $0.015 per thousand output tokens) **marked up by 30%**. If credits are exhausted, the scan is refused. This is the only paid external call in the purchase-order cycle.

> The module **charges nothing** directly via Stripe or QuickBooks. The "Create invoice" button creates an invoice **as a draft**; it is Accounting (Module 15) that posts it and, where applicable, exports it to QuickBooks.

| Rule | Effect |

| Out-of-bounds line quantity (must be greater than 0, very high cap) | Refused |
| Negative unit price | Refused |

### 5.2 FAQ

**Where is the "Purchase Orders" menu?**
It does not exist. Purchase orders are the **"Orders"** tab of the **Store** menu (`/magasin`), which is the tab open by default. The supplier-records tab is called "Supplier" (singular).

**Does receiving a PO update stock automatically?**
Yes. Moving a PO to **Received** (then Save) increases the stock of the linked products and creates one inbound movement per product. It is atomic and serialized (never double-counted).

**Can I receive only part of a PO?**
No. Receiving is **total**: the whole PO is received at once. There is no received quantity per line nor staggered receipts. If you receive only part of it, adjust the lines **before** receiving, or do the receiving at final delivery and correct stock with manual movements.

**I received a PO by mistake, how do I roll it back?**
You cannot un-validate a "Received" PO (the system returns an error to protect inventory). Correct **inventory** by **cancelling the generated stock movements** (Movements tab → "Cancel this movement"), which creates reversing movements. The PO stays "Received" for audit.

**Does receiving update my products' cost price?**
No. It records a cost **on the movement** (audit and accounting) and updates the **quantity** in stock, but it **does not touch** the cost price on the product record. There is no moving average cost; the cost price stays entered by hand.

**Why can I no longer edit a PO's lines?**
Because it is **Received** or **Invoiced**. The lines then lock (editing the subtotal after a receipt would distort stock and accounting). Correct the lines before receiving or invoicing.

**Why is deletion refused?**
A **Received**, **Invoiced** or already **posted** PO cannot be deleted (traceability). For a posted PO, first reverse its general-ledger entry (Module 15). A plain draft, though, deletes without a problem.

**Does sending by email change the status?**
Yes, but **only** from Draft to Sent. A PO already Confirmed, In progress, Received, etc. keeps its status when sent.

**What are the "Confirmed" and "In progress" statuses for?**
For your tracking. They are valid and displayed (blue and purple), but **no automatic action** triggers them: you set them by hand in the status menu.

**Does the supplier need an account to view the PO?**
No. Sending creates a **public link** valid for 90 days, which shows the PO read-only (with its prices, totals and notes) without authentication. The link becomes inaccessible if the PO is cancelled or after expiration.

**Does the AI scan create a PO directly?**
No. It **extracts** the lines into the creation window; you review, correct, then **create** the PO. The scan consumes AI credits.

**How are POs from a request for quote generated?**
By **awarding**: in the request for quote, you select suppliers, then "Award → generate PO". The system creates one PO (Draft) per selected supplier, with the priced lines only. Awarding requires a manager or storekeeper role (a plain user cannot award, even though they can create POs by hand).

**Is a transmitted PO posted without me creating an invoice?**
Yes, potentially. The accounting sync records a purchase entry for any PO **beyond draft** with an amount. Creating an invoice is therefore not the only path to accounting; it is a consequence to be aware of.

**Can I change a PO's supplier?**
Yes, as long as the PO is not locked (Received / Invoiced): the supplier name is then updated. But in practice, it is better to create a new PO for a different supplier.

### 5.3 What does not exist (known limitations)

- No "Purchase Orders" or "Purchasing" menu entry (it is the "Orders" tab of the Store).
- No partial or staggered receiving (total and single receipt).
- No "un-receiving": a receipt is corrected by cancelling the stock movements.
- No workflow buttons (Confirm, In progress…): status by hand, except sending (Draft → Sent) and invoicing (→ Invoiced).
- No moving weighted average cost: the product's cost price is not recalculated on receipt.
- No more than two taxes, and they are frozen at PO creation.
- No deletion of a received, invoiced or posted PO.
- No PO duplication (recreate by hand).
- The QuickBooks CSV is a simplified export, generated in the browser.

## 6. Summary

- **Purchase orders** are the **"Orders"** tab of the **Store** menu (`/magasin`, Operations section), the tab open by default. **There is no "Purchase Orders" menu.** The supplier tab is called "Supplier" (singular).
- **Create a PO**: by hand ("Supplier order" button), by **AI scan** (image or PDF, read-only, AI credits) or by **awarding** a request for quote (one PO per selected supplier).
- **Number** `BC-00012` (five digits, concurrency-safe). **Supplier required.**
- **Seven statuses**: Draft → Sent → Confirmed → In progress → **Received** → Invoiced, plus Cancelled. Statuses set by hand, **except** sending (Draft → Sent) and invoicing (→ Invoiced).
- **Rich detail panel**: information, delivery, items (with selection from inventory), totals (subtotal before tax + two frozen taxes + tax-included total), notes, and actions (HTML, Preview, Send, QuickBooks CSV, Excel, Create invoice, Delete, Save). **Targeted save**: only the changed fields go to the server.
- **Sending to the supplier**: themed email + **90-day public link** (viewable without an account); moves the PO to Sent if it was in Draft.
- **Receiving** ("Received" status + Save): **increases the stock** and creates a costed inbound movement per product, **atomically** and **serialized**. **Final**: you correct a mistake by cancelling the movements, not by un-validating the PO. The **product's cost price is not** recalculated.
- **Locks**: lines not editable after Received / Invoiced; deletion refused for a Received, Invoiced or posted PO.
- **Invoicing**: "Create invoice" generates a **draft** supplier invoice and moves the PO to Invoiced. Moreover, a PO **transmitted with an amount** is **automatically posted** to the general ledger on sync.
- **Permissions**: PO write for admin / super-admin / manager / storekeeper / **user**; **awarding** a request for quote requires manager or storekeeper (not the plain user); employee and accountant are read-only.
- **Money effect**: automatic posting to the general ledger (as soon as a PO is transmitted with an amount) + AI credits for the scan (actual token cost marked up by 30%). No direct Stripe payment.
