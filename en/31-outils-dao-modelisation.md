# Module 31 — CAD / 3D Modeling (cao2)

> **Version**: 2.1 (written from the actual source code, revision 2026-07)
> **Reference code**: `frontend/src/pages/Cao2Page.tsx` + the entire `frontend/src/components/cao2/` folder (toolbar `Cao2Toolbar`, 3D scene `Cao2Scene`, 2D plan `Cao2Plan2D`, sheet layout `Cao2Sheet`, panels, menus, furniture catalog `Cao2ItemCatalog`), `frontend/src/i18n/locales/fr/cao2.json` (labels); **backend** = `backend/routers/cao.py` (persistence + rendering, 6 endpoints), `backend/routers/cao_ai.py` (drawing assistant, 2 endpoints), `backend/gemini_image.py` (image engine). **8 endpoints in total**; actual backend prefix `/api/erp/v1/cao` (not to be confused with the frontend route `/cao2`).
> **PostgreSQL tables**: `cao_documents` (**per tenant**, JSONB column `model_json`, created on demand the first time). AI billing relies on the shared tables `public.ai_prepaid_credits` (credit balance), `public.ai_usage_tracking` (usage tracking) and `public.entreprises`.
> **Scope**: a **web-native workshop for 3D modeling and 2D sheet layout** of a residential building, directly in the browser, with no external software. You draw walls, rooms, slabs, roofs, openings (doors / windows), stairs and furniture; you dimension; you lay out a sheet (sheet + PDF); you produce a **photorealistic AI render**; you **derive a takeoff into a quote**; and an **AI assistant** can draw from a plain-language description in English or from an imported plan (PDF / image). This module is **not** the **Takeoff** module (quantity takeoff on PDF, module 30), nor the **PDF3D / Hover** module (its neighbor in the same 3D DESIGN section). It carries a **permanent "in development" banner**: the described features are in place and functional, but the tool is still evolving.

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 The module's mission

The CAD module lets you **model a building in 3D** and derive from it, in the same workshop:

- an **envelope** (walls, slabs, roof) and its layout (doors, windows, stairs, furniture);
- **automatic quantities** — areas (rooms, slabs, roof footprints) and lengths (walls) — for a summary takeoff;
- a **2D sheet layout** (dimensioned floor plan, title block, scale) exportable as a **vector PDF**;
- a **photorealistic presentation image** generated by AI for a client;
- a **budgetary estimate** converted directly into a **quote** (Sales module);
- an **export of the 3D model** (GLB / STL / OBJ) to another application.

The model stays **editable at all times**: the 3D view, the 2D view and the sheet layout share the same model. A change made in one view is visible in the others.

### 1.2 Key concepts

- **Model hierarchy**: `Site` → `Building` → `Level` → elements (`Wall`, `Slab`, `Roof`, `Stair`, furniture `Element`). A `Door` or a `Window` is the child of a `Wall` (or of a vertical face of a roof-shape).
- **Active level**: every new element is created in the **active level**. You make a level active by clicking it in the scene tree; an "active" badge marks it. Levels are **stacked** in 3D (default height 2.7 m); in the 2D view and on the sheet, only the active level is drawn.
- **Shared model, no database**: the model lives in a browser-side state store (the "Pascal core"). **No database writes** occur while you draw; only **saving a document** and the **billed AI calls** talk to the server.
- **Three views of the same model** (the toolbar's "View" group):

| View | Label | Content | Component |
|-----|---------|---------|-----------|
| **3D** (default) | "3D view" | Real-time perspective: drawing, selection, navigation (orbit, view cube, walk mode), realistic render, AI render, 3D export. | `Cao2Scene` |
| **2D** | "2D view (plan)" | Interactive top-down plan (SVG): 2D drawing and selection, sketch (linework). | `Cao2Plan2D` |
| **Plan** | "Plan" | **Static** sheet layout (ARCH D paper size): dimensioned floor plan, title block, notes, PDF export. | `Cao2Sheet` |

### 1.3 Access

- **Left sidebar**: **3D DESIGN** section → **CAD** (cube icon). The same section contains **PDF3D (Hover)** and **3D Render**, which are separate modules.
- **Route**: `/cao2` (protected page, authentication required). The old `/cao` route **redirects automatically** to `/cao2`.
- **Page**: `Cao2Page.tsx`. The workshop reuses the visual style of the Takeoff module (no styling of its own); normal height **82 vh**, **100 vh** in fullscreen.
- A **permanent banner** "CAD - Construction workshop (in development)" is shown at the top.

### 1.4 Permissions and roles

- **Any authenticated user** of the tenant can list, create, modify and delete CAD documents, produce renders and use the AI assistant. **No role-specific restriction** in this module (no "administrator", "accountant" or "super-administrator" guard).
- **Documents** are private to your company (isolation by PostgreSQL schema: one tenant never sees another's documents).
- **Read-only mode** (inherited from the tenant's account) and the **super-administrator** exemption for AI credits are handled centrally by the authentication layer, not redefined in this module.

### 1.5 AI models and billing

Three of the module's features consume the tenant's **AI credits**, re-billed with a **30% margin (× 1.30)**:

| Feature | Model | Where | Ref |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Draw Assistant** (plain language) | Claude Sonnet 4.6 | Draw Assistant panel | `cao_ai.py:378` |
| **Import a plan → 3D** (vision) | Claude Opus 4.8 | Assistant's "Import a plan" button | `cao_ai.py:646` |
| **Photorealistic render** | Google Gemini (server key) | "AI render" modal | `cao.py:539` |

The **estimate-to-quote** ("Estimate this model") uses the AI Estimation of the Quotes module (also billed in credits). The API key stays **server-side** in every case; the client only sends its authentication token. In self-hosted mode (billing disabled) or for a super-administrator, these calls remain **free but tracked**.

### 1.6 Sub-modules and panels

| Element | Role |
|---|---|
| **Toolbar** (`Cao2Toolbar`) | File, drawing tools, drawing settings, history, levels, view, display, navigation, fullscreen, reset. |
| **Scene tree** (`Cao2SceneTree`, left) | Model hierarchy, selection, visibility, active level. |

| **Furniture catalog** (`Cao2ItemCatalog`) | 110 3D objects by category. |

**2.2.1 File group**

- **Document menu** (`Cao2DocMenu`): the button shows the current document's name (or "Untitled document") and a **status badge** (see 2.11). Items: **New**; **Save** (or **Save as...** if the document has no name yet); **Rename** (grayed out with no document); then the **Open** list (each row: select, or delete with confirmation). Prompts go through browser dialogs ("Document name:", "New name:", delete confirmation).
- **Export menu** (`Cao2ExportMenu`, contextual by view): **3D model** section (GLB / STL / OBJ, active in 3D view); **Sheet layout** section (PDF, active in Plan view); **Image** section (AI render, active in 3D view). Items outside their view are **grayed out**.
- **Draw Assistant** (toggle button, sparkle icon): opens or closes the drawing AI panel.

**2.2.2 Tools group**

| Tool | Label | Role |
|-------|---------|------|
| Select | "Select" | Select / move an element (default tool). |
| Wall | "Wall" | Draw a wall (2 clicks + keyboard length entry). |
| Rectangle | "Rectangle" | Draw a room = 4 closed walls (2 clicks). |
| Slab | "Slab" | Draw a polygonal floor. |
| Roof | "Roof ▾" | Menu: Auto roof (over walls) / Roof (shape). |
| Door | "Door" | Cut a door by clicking a wall. |
| Window | "Window" | Cut a window by clicking a wall. |
| Stair | "Stair" | Place a stair. |
| Furniture | "Furniture" | Open the catalog and place a piece of furniture. |
| Dimension | "Dimension" | Manual dimensioning (see limitations, section 5). |
| Adjust | "Adjust walls" | Extend or trim a wall to the nearest wall. |
| Sketch | "Sketch ▾" | 2D sketch menu (grayed out outside the 2D view). |

The **Roof menu** (`Cao2RoofMenu`) offers two modes: **Auto roof (over walls)** — a multi-slope skeleton generated automatically over the footprint of the active level's walls — and **Roof (shape)** — a roof-shape drawn from a 2-corner rectangle, with 7 parametric shapes.

The **Sketch menu** (`Cao2SketchMenu`, 2D view only) contains a **Draw** section (Line, Circle, Arc, Rectangle, Point) and a **Modify** section (Move, Copy, Rotate, Mirror — grayed out until a sketch entity is selected).

**2.2.3 Drawing settings group**

- **Angle 15°** (toggle): constrains the drawing angle to a multiple of 15°. **On by default.**
- **Align** (toggle): orthogonal alignment magnet to existing walls. **On by default.**
- **Thickness** of new walls: two presets — **"Ext. 9 1/4""** (exterior wall, 0.235 m) and **"Partition 4 1/2""** (interior partition, 0.114 m).
- **Height** of new walls: a dropdown of 5 standard Quebec heights — 8'2", 9'2", 10'2", 11'2", 12'2" (default 8'2").

**2.2.4 History and Levels groups**

- **Undo / Redo**: undo stack; the buttons are disabled when the stack is empty.
- **Add level**: creates a new level that becomes **active** and selected (default height 2.7 m).

**2.2.5 View group**

- **3D / 2D / Plan**: toggle between the three views.
- **Parallel** (3D view only): orthographic (no-perspective) projection; disabled in walk mode.
- **Walk** (3D view only): first-person navigation.

**2.2.6 Display group** (`Cao2DisplayMenu`)

A menu of checkboxes:

- **Wall dimensions** (on by default), **Slab areas** (on), **Rooms (areas)** (on), **Floor grid** (on).
- **Edges (3D)** (3D view, off by default): highlights the outlines of the volumes.
- **Realistic render** (3D view, on by default): realistic lighting while keeping the model editable.
- **Units** section: **m** (metric) or **ft-in** (imperial feet-inches).

**2.2.7 Navigation, fullscreen, reset**

- **Zoom out / Zoom in / Fit to screen**.
- **Fullscreen** (toggle).
- **Reset** (isolated on the right, in muted red): clears the scene after the confirmation "Reset the CAD scene? This action cannot be undone."

### 2.3 Scene tree (left panel)

The tree (`Cao2SceneTree`, title "Scene tree"; "Empty scene" if the model is empty) shows the entire hierarchy as an expandable outline. Each row has an expand / collapse chevron, an icon per type, a label (name or translated type), an "active" badge on the current level, and an eye button to **Show / Hide**.

- **Selection**: a click selects the element; **Ctrl / Cmd + click** adds to a **multi-selection**.
- **Active level**: clicking a **level** makes it **active** (target of new elements) and selects it.
- **Keyboard**: `Enter` / `Space` selects, arrow keys navigate and expand / collapse.
- **Safeguard**: the root containers **Site** and **Building** are **not deletable** from the keyboard.

Displayed types: Site, Building, Level, Wall, Slab, Roof, Stair, Door, Window, Element.

### 2.4 Properties panel (right panel)

The "Properties" panel (`Cao2PropertiesPanel`, collapsible into a 32 px vertical tab) shows fields **by the type** of the selected element. Length fields follow the active **unit system**; in imperial, the entry accepts the feet-inches-sixteenths format (shown hint: "e.g. 8-2-7" = 8 ft 2 in 7/16, or simply "8").

**Special display cases**

- **No selection** → the panel switches to the **takeoff (Quantities)** (see 2.5).
- **Multi-selection (2 or more elements of the same type)**: walls → Height (+ 5 presets) and Thickness (+ 2 presets), with the note "mixed values" if they differ; doors / windows → Width and Height; **Delete (N)** button.
- **Sketch entity**: circle / arc → Radius; line → Length (read-only); rectangle → Width / Height (read-only); Delete button.

**Fields by element type**

| Type | Fields |
|------|--------|
| **Wall** | 3D handles (Move / Rotate, in 3D), Length (editable), Height (+ presets), Thickness (+ Ext. / Partition presets), **Top slope (x/12)** + highest side (gable / raked wall), **Composition** section (assembly). |
| **Slab** | Elevation (signed), Area (read-only). |
| **Roof (shape)** | **Roof type** (7 shapes, see 4.5), Wall height, Pitch (°), Overhang, Width, Depth, 3D handles + Position X / Z + Rotation. |
| **Auto roof** | **Roof type** (3 shapes: Gable / Hip / Flat), **Gables per edge** (checkboxes, rectangular footprint only), Pitch, Overhang, Fascia. |
| **Stair** | Segment type (**Flight** / **Landing**), Width, Length, Height, **Steps**, 3D handles, Position X / Z, Rotation. |
| **Furniture** | Model (read-only), **Attachment** Floor / Wall / Ceiling (read-only), 3D handles, Position X / Z / Y (height), Rotation. |
| **Door / Window** | **Opening type** (QC preset), Width, Height, "Distance from left"; window → **Align to header** (checkbox) + **Sill height**. |
| **Roof opening** (on a gable face) | Type, Width, Height, "Position along the face", "Height on the face". |
| **Level** | Level (index), Storey height. |

Every element (except doors / windows) has a common **Name** field; walls, slabs, roofs and stairs have a **Material** selector; all have a **Delete** button.

**Material**: a **Default** preset, plus 9 named swatches (White, Wood, Brick, Concrete, Plaster, Tile, Marble, Metal, Glass) and a **Custom color** (color picker).

**Wall composition** (`Cao2WallAssemblySection`): an **Assembly type** menu (None, or a library: "Exterior wall, lap siding (R-24.5)", "2x4 partition") shows the list of layers (label + thickness + R-value), the **Total thickness** and the **Total R** (read-only).

### 2.5 Quantities panel (takeoff)

When nothing is selected, the right panel shows the **summary takeoff** (`Cao2QuantitiesPanel`, title "Quantities"), derived from the model:

- **Rooms** (count + area), **Walls** (count + length), **Slabs** (count + area), **Roofs** (count + footprint), **Doors**, **Windows**, **Roof openings** (if the count is greater than 0), **Stairs**.
- A **"Material takeoff" section** for assembly walls appears automatically when relevant.
- Note: "Summary takeoff derived from the model. Use 'Estimate this model' to turn it into a quote."
- Blue **"Estimate this model"** button (grayed out if the model is not estimable): opens the estimate modal (see 2.6).

### 2.6 "Estimate this model" modal

The `Cao2EstimateModal` modal turns the takeoff into a **quote**, in two steps:

1. **Takeoff summary** (rooms / walls / slabs / roofs / openings / stairs) then a **"Generate AI estimate"** button (note: "Generation uses AI and deducts a cost from your credits").
2. **Preview table** — columns **Description**, **Qty**, **Unit price**, **Amount** — followed by the totals:

| Line | Value |
|-------|--------|
| Subtotal | sum of the lines |
| Administration | **3%** (fixed) |
| Contingencies | **12%** (fixed) |
| Profit | **15%** (fixed) |
| Estimated total (before tax) | subtotal + the 3 markups |

The estimate covers a **complete build on a per-square-foot basis** (all trades: structure, foundation, envelope, insulation, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, interior, finishes; the 3D takeoff refines the envelope). A **warning** appears if the floor area is undetermined (no closed room or slab) — in that case, close the wall outline or add a slab for a more reliable estimate.

**Regenerate** / **Create quote** buttons. On creation, a success screen shows "Quote {number} created" and an **"Open the quote"** button that navigates to the Sales module (`/devis?open=id`). Internal locks prevent a double call and duplicate quote creation.

### 2.7 Draw Assistant (AI)

The `Cao2AiPanel` panel (the "Draw Assistant" button) is a chat. It shows an intro ("Describe what you want to draw and I'll prepare a plan to confirm.") and three examples:

- "A 4 m by 5 m room"
- "Add a gable roof over the walls"
- "A wall from (0,0) to (6,0)"

An input area (`Enter` sends; `Shift + Enter` inserts a new line) and an **"Import a plan (PDF/image) → 3D"** button. A disclaimer reminds you: "AI can make mistakes; check the preview before confirming."

The AI answers with a **"Plan preview"**: the **list of proposed operations** (e.g. "Room 4 x 5 m (4 walls + floor)", "Wall from (0, 0) to (6, 0)", "Roof over the active level walls", "Gable roof …"). Nothing is drawn until you click **"Draw it"**; **"Cancel"** discards the plan. Execution happens **browser-side**, in the active level.

**v1 scope**: the assistant draws **walls, rooms and roofs** only — **not** openings, stairs or furniture (that will be "Draw v2"). The preview is **textual** (no 3D ghost). The assistant is **single-conversation** within the session (no persistent history in the panel).

### 2.8 Photorealistic render modal (AI render)

Opened from the **Export → AI render** menu (3D view). The `Cao2RenderModal` modal shows:

- **Captured view**: the preview of the current 3D view (message "Capture unavailable (switch to 3D view)" if you are not in 3D).
- **Details and description** (optional, up to 2,000 characters): e.g. "red brick siding, grey shingle roofing, late-day sky, landscaping with trees, a car in the driveway…".
- **Quality**: **Pro** (best quality), **Standard** (balanced) or **Fast** (economical).
- **Resolution**: **2K** (fast, sharp) or **4K** (ultra-detailed, Pro / Standard only — disabled in Fast).
- **Generate render** button; then, on the produced image, **Download** or **Regenerate**.

A synchronous lock prevents double billing from a double-click. A **strictly positive** credit balance is required (see 4.3).

### 2.9 Furniture catalog

The `Cao2ItemCatalog` modal opens with the **Furniture** tool:

- **Category** tabs: **Furniture**, **Appliance**, **Bathroom**, **Kitchen**, **Outdoor**.
- A **Search** field (by name) and a grid of thumbnails.
- **110 objects** in total (furniture 49, appliances 22, outdoor 15, kitchen 14, bathroom 10), as 3D models (GLB) loaded from an **external CDN** (Pascal's public Supabase).
- Shown hint: "Pick a piece, then click in the 3D scene to place it on the floor."

### 2.10 Plan view (sheet layout) and title block

The **Plan** view (`Cao2Sheet` + `SheetTitleBlock`) is a **static sheet** (neither panning nor zoom) in **ARCH D** (24 × 36 in) landscape format. It contains a large **PLAN** (wall and slab framing + **automatic dimensioning**), an **isometric view** and **4 elevations** North / East / South / West (partial content, marked "coming soon").

The **title block** is editable and **persisted** with the document: Project, Client, Drawing title, Sheet no., Date, Drawn by, **Scale** (computed, read-only), **North (deg)**. Added to it are a **Revisions** table (No. / Date / Description, with add and delete) and a **General notes** block.

The **vector PDF export** of the sheet is done via the **Export → PDF (sheet layout)** menu; the file is generated browser-side.

## 3. Step-by-step workflows

### 3.1 Start a new plan

1. Open **3D DESIGN → CAD**. A demo scene appears.
2. To start from scratch: **Document → New** menu (or the **Reset** button on the right, with confirmation).
3. Check the **active level** in the scene tree (the "active" badge): everything you draw goes into that level.
4. If needed, choose the **units** (Display menu → m / ft-in). Default: **feet-inches**.

### 3.2 Draw a wall

1. Click the **Wall** tool.
2. **1st click** in the view: start point. The cursor snaps to notable points (corners, midpoints), to other walls (the "Align" magnet) and to the grid.
3. Move: a preview of the wall follows the cursor, with its length shown.
4. **2nd click**: end point. The wall is created.

**Keyboard entry of the exact length**: while drawing, **type the length** on the keyboard (a badge shows the value). In feet-inches: `12-6` = 12 ft 6 in; `8-2-7` = 8 ft 2 in 7/16. In metric: `3.5`. Use the **arrow keys** to lock the direction, then **Enter** to place the wall at the exact length (**Esc** cancels the entry).

Set the **Thickness** (Ext. 9 1/4" / Partition 4 1/2") and the **Height** (8'2" to 12'2") as needed before drawing.

### 3.3 Draw a room (rectangle of walls)

1. Click the **Rectangle** tool.
2. **1st click**: first corner. **2nd click**: opposite corner.
3. Four closed walls are created at once.

### 3.4 Sloped wall (raked top)

1. Select the wall.
2. In **Properties**, set the **Top slope** (from 1/12 to 12/12) and the **highest side**.
3. The wall stays vertical; only the top edge tilts, in 2D as in 3D.

### 3.5 Place a slab

1. Click the **Slab** tool.
2. Click the polygon's vertices in turn (floor).
3. **Double-click** or **Enter** closes the slab (minimum 3 vertices).
4. The area is computed automatically (shown at the center if "Slab areas" is on).

### 3.6 Place a door or a window

1. Click the **Door** or **Window** tool.
2. **Click directly on a wall** (its surface, in 3D or 2D): the opening is cut at the clicked point, with a default preset. Clicking **off** a wall does nothing.
3. Adjust in **Properties**: Opening type (preset), Width, Height, "Distance from left". For a window: **Sill height** and **Align to header**.

### 3.7 Opening on a gable (roof-shape)

You can place doors / windows on the **vertical faces** (gable walls) of a roof-shape:

1. **Door** or **Window** tool active.
2. Click a **vertical face** (gable) of the roof-shape. Clicking the **slope** is rejected (message "Place the opening on a vertical face (gable wall).").
3. Edit in Properties: Type, Width, Height, "Position along the face", "Height on the face".

### 3.8 Add a roof

**A. Auto roof (over walls)** — via the **Roof → Auto roof** menu:

1. Choose **Roof → Auto roof**.
2. Click in the view: a sloped roof is generated automatically over **the footprint of the active level's walls** (multi-slope skeleton, with valleys on L-shaped plans).
3. In Properties: Type (Gable / Hip / Flat), Pitch, Overhang, Fascia, and **Gables per edge** (checkboxes) — only if the footprint is **rectangular**.

**B. Roof (shape)** — via the **Roof → Roof (shape)** menu:

1. Choose **Roof → Roof (shape)**.
2. **1st click** then **2nd click**: draw the footprint rectangle.
3. In Properties: choose the **shape** among 7 (see 4.5), set Pitch, Overhang, Wall height, Width, Depth.
4. The roof-shape **moves / rotates** with a 3D handle (Move / Rotate toggle, or Position X / Z and Rotation fields).

### 3.9 Add a stair

1. Click the **Stair** tool.
2. Click in the view: a stair is created at the clicked point.
3. In Properties: Segment type (**Flight** / **Landing**), Width, Length, Height, **Steps**, Position and Rotation.

### 3.10 Place furniture

1. Click the **Furniture** tool: the **catalog** opens.
2. Choose a **category** or **search** by name.
3. Click a piece of furniture: it becomes the object to place and the catalog closes.
4. **Click in the 3D scene**: the piece is placed on the floor at the clicked point.
5. Select the piece to show a **3D handle** (Move / Rotate) and set Position X / Z / Y and Rotation. The **Attachment** (Floor / Wall / Ceiling) is determined at placement.

### 3.11 Manage levels

1. **Add level** button: creates a level, makes it **active** and selects it.
2. Levels are **stacked** in 3D (default storey height 2.7 m, adjustable in Properties). In 2D / plan, only the **active level** is shown.
3. Clicking a level in the scene tree makes it active.

### 3.12 Navigate in 3D

- **Orbit** (default): drag with the right button = rotate around the model; `Shift` = pan sideways.
- **Wheel**: zoom; or the **Zoom in / out / Fit to screen** buttons.
- **View cube** (corner of the view): click a face = orthogonal view; a corner = isometric view. A **N/E/S/W compass** at the foot of the cube rotates with the view.
- **Parallel**: toggle perspective ↔ orthographic (3D view only).
- **Walk (first person)**: the **Walk** button; **click the view** to lock the mouse; **arrow keys** or **WASD / ZQSD** = forward / back / strafe (eye height ~1.7 m); **Esc** to exit.

### 3.13 Materials and realistic render

- **Material**: in Properties, pick a preset (White, Wood, Brick, Concrete, Plaster, Tile, Marble, Metal, Glass) or a **custom color**.
- **Realistic render** (Display menu, 3D view, on by default): realistic lighting (procedural ambience and sky, cast shadow), with the model **staying editable**. Turn it off for a faster "flat" render.
- **Edges (3D)**: highlights the outlines of the volumes (off by default).

### 3.14 Save, open, rename, delete a document

Everything goes through the **Document** menu:

- **New**: blank scene.
- **Save** / **Save as...**: if the document has no name, a prompt asks for it.
- **Rename** / **Delete**: on the current document (confirmation for deletion).
- **Open**: list of your company's documents.
- **Auto-save**: the document is saved in the background after ~2 s of inactivity (the state is shown next to the menu). Documents are stored server-side, isolated per tenant.

### 3.15 Use the Draw Assistant (AI)

1. **Draw Assistant** button: the chat panel opens.
2. Describe what you want ("A 4 m by 5 m room", "Add a gable roof over the walls"…).
3. The AI answers with a **Plan preview** (list of operations).
4. **Draw it** applies the plan in the active level; **Cancel** discards it.

> Reminder: the v1 assistant draws walls, rooms and roofs; it **consumes AI credits**.

### 3.16 Import a plan (PDF / image) → 3D

1. In the Draw Assistant panel, the **"Import a plan (PDF/image) → 3D"** button.
2. Choose a file (PDF, PNG, JPEG, GIF or WEBP). The vision AI (Claude Opus) reads the floor plan, computes a scale and extracts the geometry.
3. The AI returns an **operations plan** (walls / rooms / roof) to **confirm** like a regular drawing.

> **Billed** feature; the result is a **draft to be reviewed** (a vector plan gives much better results than a scanned plan). Distinct from the **PDF3D / Hover** module.

### 3.17 Generate a photorealistic render (AI render)

1. Switch to **3D view** and frame the camera.
2. **Export → AI render** menu: the modal opens with the view preview.
3. (Optional) Add a **description** (cladding, roofing, ambience, landscape…).
4. Choose **Quality** (Pro / Standard / Fast) and **Resolution** (2K / 4K).
5. **Generate render**, then **Download** or **Regenerate**.

> **Billing**: consumes AI credits; a **strictly positive** balance is required (otherwise the render is refused, code 402).

### 3.18 Lay out the sheet and export to PDF

1. **View → Plan** tab: the sheet (ARCH D, 24 × 36 in, landscape) appears.
2. The floor plan is drawn automatically (walls and slabs, automatic dimensions, opening symbols, roof edges, north arrow).
3. Fill in the **title block** (title, number, date, drafter), the **Revisions** and the **Notes**: these fields are **persisted** with the document.
4. **Export → PDF (sheet layout)** menu: generates a vector PDF of the sheet.

### 3.19 Export the 3D model

In **3D view**, the **Export** menu:

- **GLB (.glb)** — complete 3D model (geometry + materials).
- **STL (.stl)** — mesh, for 3D printing.
- **OBJ (.obj)** — mesh + materials, interoperable.

### 3.20 Estimate the model and create a quote

1. Deselect everything (the **Quantities** panel appears).
2. Click **"Estimate this model"**: the estimate modal opens on the takeoff summary.
3. **Generate AI estimate**: the AI produces a table of quote lines (by trade), marked up Administration 3% / Contingencies 12% / Profit 15%.
4. **Create quote**, then **Open the quote** in the Sales module.

### 4.3 AI billing (calculations)

Tenant margin applied to all calls: **× 1.30**.

| Call | Cost (before margin) | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **AI render** (`/cao/render`) | image tokens × price/token (actual), otherwise a flat rate per tier (Pro / Standard / Fast) | **Strictly positive** balance required (402); endpoint **shared** with the 3D Render module |
| **Draw chat** (`/cao/ai/chat`) | (input × 0.003 + output × 0.015) / 1000 | Sonnet model; tolerates a negative balance (post-paid) |
| **Plan import** (`/cao/ai/plan-to-3d`) | (input × 0.005 + cache write × 0.00625 + cache read × 0.0005 + output × 0.025) / 1000 | Opus model, pricing that accounts for the cache |
| **Estimate → quote** | billed by the Quotes module's AI Estimation | Fixed markups Administration 3% / Contingencies 12% / Profit 15% |

> The `/cao/render` endpoint is **the same code** as the **3D Render** module's (`/rendu-3d`): the `source` field only changes the billing label (`cao_render` or `render3d`). There is **no server-side idempotency key** on the paid endpoints; the render's anti-double-click lock is **browser-side**.

## 5. Integrations and FAQ

### 5.1 Integrations with other modules

- **Sales / Quotes (module 08)**: the **"Estimate this model"** button derives a quote from the 3D takeoff (number `DEV-YYYY-NNN`), then "Open the quote" opens the Sales module. The bridge is built by composing existing endpoints of the Quotes module (no endpoint dedicated to CAD).
- **Takeoff (module 30)**: a **separate** tool. Takeoff does **quantity takeoff on a PDF plan (2D)**; CAD **builds a 3D model** and derives its own takeoff from it. The two share utilities (imperial entry, dimensioning).
- **PDF3D / Hover**: a **separate** module (a neighbor in the 3D DESIGN section) that converts a plan to 3D via the external Hover service. Not to be confused with CAD's "Import a plan → 3D" feature (which, for its part, uses Claude's vision AI).
- **3D Render (`/rendu-3d`)**: a separate module that reuses **the same** render endpoint as CAD. The result and the billing are of the same nature.

### 5.2 Known limitations (verified in the code)

- **"In development" banner**: the module is still evolving.
- **AI assistant (v1)**: draws **walls, rooms, roofs** only (no openings, stairs or furniture); **textual** preview; single-conversation within the session.
- **"Dimension" tool**: dimensioning is **automatic** on the plan / the sheet; the manual dimension tool is not fully complete yet.
- **Plan view (sheet)**: **static** (no panning or zoom). Only the **floor plan** is fully rendered; the **elevations** (N / E / S / W) and the **isometric view** are partly "coming soon". Section / detail markers and multi-sheet: not implemented.
- **Roof gables**: reliable on a **rectangular** footprint only. On an L / T / U plan, the module produces an automatic **hip**; for true gables, place a **Roof (shape)** per wing.
- **Roof openings**: placeable only on the **vertical (gable) faces** of a roof-shape; a click on the slope is rejected. They are visible in 3D and on the sheet / PDF, but not yet in the interactive 2D view.
- **Furniture**: depends on an **external CDN** (public Supabase); without network access to that CDN, the models won't load.
- **Idempotency**: no server-side idempotency key on the paid endpoints; the anti-double-billing lock is browser-side.

### 5.3 FAQ

**Q. What happened to the old CAD module (`/cao`)?**
It was replaced by CAD (`/cao2`); the old address redirects automatically.

**Q. What is the default unit?**
The **foot-inch** (imperial). Switch to meters via the Display menu → Units.

**Q. I click to cut a window and nothing happens.**
Click **directly on a wall's surface** (not in empty space). On a roof-shape, click a **vertical face** (gable) — the slope is rejected.

**Q. Is my drawing saved automatically?**
Yes, ~2 s after your last action (see the state next to the Document menu). You can also save manually.

**Q. Are the AI render or the estimate free?**
No: the **AI render**, **Import a plan → 3D**, the **Draw Assistant** and the **Estimate → quote** all consume your company's **AI credits** (re-billed with a 30% margin). The render additionally requires a **strictly positive** balance.

**Q. Can I have the AI draw my whole building?**
Partially: the v1 assistant draws walls, rooms and roofs. Openings, stairs and furniture are placed by hand (dedicated tools).

**Q. Why does my roof go flat when I check a gable edge on an L-shaped plan?**
Per-edge gables are reliable only on a **rectangular** footprint. On an L / T / U, use the **Roof (shape)** tool per wing, or leave the automatic **hip**.

**Q. The new level doesn't receive my walls.**
Check the **active level** in the scene tree (the "active" badge): new elements go into the active level. Click the desired level to activate it.

**Q. How do I get a realistic image for a client?**
3D view → Export → AI render → choose quality / resolution → Generate → Download. An AI credit balance is required.

**Q. How do I get my model into other software?**
3D view → Export → GLB / STL / OBJ.

**Q. Does the sheet layout include the elevations?**
The floor plan is complete and dimensioned; the elevations and the isometric view are partly reserved ("coming soon").
