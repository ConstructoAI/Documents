It is **not** a route-optimization system, nor a PDF delivery-note generator, nor a push-notification system. Several capabilities exist on the server side but **without a user interface** (delivery items, equipment reservations, vehicle trips, GPS zone creation, alert generation): see section 1.3.

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Module mission

Give a Quebec construction company a **single register of site logistics**: knowing what has to be delivered and when, which equipment and vehicles are available or under maintenance, which coordination activities are planned in the field (concrete pour, crane arrival, street closure), and where the fleet is based on the latest GPS positions.

Concretely, the module answers questions such as:

- "Which deliveries are scheduled this week and where should they be stored?"
- "Is this excavator available, in use, or under maintenance? When was it last inspected?"
- "How many trucks are available and what is their mileage?"
- "Which activities need to be coordinated on the site tomorrow morning?"
- "Are there any open logistics alerts?" (AI assistant)

### 1.2 What the module does (verified against the code)

- **Deliveries**: create, list (paginated, 25 per page), search, filter by status, change the status inline, and delete inbound or outbound deliveries. Each delivery receives a unique reference `LIV-#####` generated by the server.
- **Equipment**: create, list, search, filter by category and status, change the status inline, and delete mobile equipment (crane, excavator, concrete mixer, generator, etc.). Unique code `EQP-#####`.
- **Equipment maintenance**: log service records (preventive, inspection, repair, certification) under the selected piece of equipment, with cost, technician, compliance, and a **next date** that automatically updates the equipment's due date.
- **Vehicles**: create, list, search, filter by status, change the status inline, update the mileage, and delete fleet vehicles. Unique license plate.
- **Site coordination**: schedule dated activities (concrete delivery, crane arrival, pour, inspection, meeting, street closure, etc.) with a schedule, the area concerned, and a person in charge. Unique reference `COORD-#####`. **Note**: this screen is displayed under the mislabeled "Trips" tab.
- **Dashboard**: four key indicators (scheduled deliveries, available equipment, available vehicles, active alerts), three detail cards, and the list of active alerts (read-only).
- **GPS fleet tracking**: view the latest position of each vehicle, the recorded locations, the zones (geofences), and the day's routes — as **coordinate tables**, without a map.
- **Read-only AI assistant**: a natural-language chat that **reads** your actual logistics data (deliveries, equipment, vehicles, coordination, alerts) and answers, **without ever writing** or accessing payroll or employees.

### 1.3 What the module does NOT do (important limits)

> **Read this before relying on the module.** Several natural expectations are **not** covered by the interface, and some capabilities exist on the server side with no screen to use them.

- **No export, no printing, no file upload, no bulk action** anywhere in the module. No PDF delivery note, no CSV, no attachment.
- **No full editing of records.** On a delivery, a piece of equipment, a vehicle, or an activity that has already been created, the interface allows only **two** gestures: change the **status** (dropdown in the table) and **delete**. There is no window for editing the other fields (type, area, costs, notes, etc.). Correcting an address or a cost requires deleting and recreating.
- **Delivery items with no interface.** The server can attach detailed lines (description, expected and received quantities, unit, compliance) to a delivery, but **no screen** lets you enter or view them. A delivery's detail does not open.
- **Equipment reservations with no interface.** The server manages equipment reservations by period — with **overlap detection** (two reservations that overlap are refused, see 4.11) — but **no screen** lets you create or view these reservations.
- **Vehicle trips with no interface.** The server records trips (departure, return, mileage, fuel, cost), but **no screen** displays or creates them. Despite the "Trips" and "GPS / Trips" labels, the real vehicle-trip concept has **no tab**.
- **Read-only alerts.** The dashboard displays active alerts, but there is **no button** to generate them or to mark them handled. The automatic generation (maintenance, inspection, insurance expiry) and the handling exist on the server side, with no screen.
- **No map.** The GPS tab shows **latitudes and longitudes as text**, never an interactive map. The zones (geofences) and routes are read-only; only **locations** can be added.
- **No push notification** (email, browser notification, text message). Alerts live in the database and are only displayed in the dashboard.
- **No accounting or stock synchronization.** Costs (daily, monthly, fuel, maintenance) are **displayed** but never turned into accounting entries. A delivery moved to "delivered" **does not** update the stock in the Store.
- **Read-only AI assistant.** The assistant **creates, modifies, and deletes nothing**: it answers from your data, without proposing any action or writing to the database.
- **The Vehicles tab has no pagination** (the full list is loaded at once), unlike the Deliveries, Equipment, and Coordination tabs (25 per page).

- Direct URL: `/logistique`.

### 1.5 Permissions and roles

The module clearly separates **reading** (open) from **writing** (reserved for certain roles).

| Action | Who can do it |
|--------|---------------|
| **View** (all tabs, all GETs, including GPS and statistics) | Any authenticated user of the tenant (`get_current_user`). |
| **Create / change the status / delete** a delivery, a piece of equipment, a maintenance record, a vehicle, a coordination activity | Logistics write role: **administrator**, **super administrator**, **manager**, **foreman**, or **storekeeper** (`require_tenant_admin_or_role`, `erp_auth.py:720`). An owner whose account carries `is_admin` is always authorized (right re-read on the server, non-forgeable). Otherwise: **403**. |
| **Add a GPS location or zone** | **Any** authenticated user — GPS writes have **no** role guard (a known inconsistency, see 4.10). |
| **Use the AI assistant** | Any authenticated user **with AI credits** (see 1.7). |

> **Consultation mode (read-only) at the tenant level.** If the company has no active Stripe subscription (subscription cancelled or absent), the entire tenant switches to **consultation mode**: reads remain permitted, but **any** creation, modification, or deletion returns **403** (`erp_auth.py:526`). This also applies to GPS writes. See the Configuration / Subscription module to restore access.

### 1.7 Costs and billing (AI assistant only)

- **The entire register part is free**: creating and viewing deliveries, equipment, vehicles, activities, and GPS locations consumes no credit.
- **Only the AI assistant is paid.** Each message consumes the company's **prepaid AI credits**. Cost = the `claude-sonnet-4-6` model's rate (US$3 per million input tokens, US$15 per million output tokens) **plus a 30% markup**. The charge is **firm** (not best-effort) and tracked under the `logistique_chat` feature in `public.ai_usage_tracking`. An account with no credits receives a **402** error and cannot send a message; logistics consultation itself remains free. Details in section 4.8.

### 1.8 Technical architecture

```
Frontend LogistiquePage.tsx (7 tabs)
    │
    ├── Deliveries/Equipment/Vehicles/Coordination/Dashboard tabs
    │        └─ api/logistics.ts ──> secondary.py  /api/erp/v1/logistics/*   (33 endpoints + stats)
    │                                 logistics_* tables (created on demand)
    │
    ├── GPS / Trips tab
    │        └─ api/gps.ts ─────────> gps.py        /api/erp/v1/gps/*         (7 endpoints, read)
    │                                 gps_* tables (NOT created on demand)
    │
    └── AI Assistant tab
             └─ api/logistiqueAi.ts ─> logistique_ai.py /api/erp/v1/logistique/ai/chat
                                        Claude sonnet-4-6, recherche_bd tool (read-only)
                                        AI credit charge + usage tracking
```

> **Point of attention for a new tenant.** The `logistics_*` tables are created **on first use** (on the fly, `_ensure_table`): a tenant that has not yet entered anything sees counters at **0** and empty lists — this is normal. However, `gps.py` **does not** create its tables on demand: on a tenant that has never received GPS data, the GPS tab can return a server error (see FAQ 5.7).

### 2.8 AI Assistant tab

## 3. Step-by-step workflows

### 3.1 Schedule a delivery

1. **Deliveries** tab → **"New delivery"**.
2. Enter the **scheduled date** (required); as needed, the time, the type (for example "Supplier" for incoming materials), the storage area, and notes.
3. **Create**. The server assigns a `LIV-#####` reference and the status `planifiee`; the delivery appears in the table.

> There is no field for the delivery's items or for the project/supplier: this information must be recorded elsewhere (notes, Store module, Projects module).

### 3.2 Track and advance a delivery

1. **Deliveries** tab → locate the row (search or filter by status as needed).
2. In the **Status** column, open the dropdown and choose the new state: `planifiee` → `en_cours` (truck en route) → `livree` (received), or `annulee`.
3. The change is saved immediately. **No effective date** is entered automatically when moving to "delivered."

### 3.3 Delete a delivery

1. **Deliveries** tab → Delete button (trash icon) on the row.
2. Confirm "Delete delivery {ref}?". The delivery **and its items** (if any) are deleted (database cascade).

### 3.4 Register a piece of equipment

1. **Equipment** tab → **"New equipment"**.
2. Enter the **name** (required); as needed the category, the ownership type (Owned or Rented), the daily and monthly costs, the location, and notes.
3. **Create**. The server assigns an `EQP-#####` code and the status `disponible`.

### 3.5 Log a maintenance service record

1. **Equipment** tab → **click the row** of the equipment (it highlights, the maintenance section opens below).
2. **"Add service record"**.
3. Choose the type (Preventive maintenance / Inspection / Repair / Certification), enter the **date** (required), the technician, the cost, check or uncheck **Compliant**, and — importantly — fill in **Next date** if you want to trigger a future alert.
4. **Create**. The service record is added to the history; if "Next date" is filled in, the equipment's `prochaine_maintenance` due date is updated.

> To delete a service record: trash button in the maintenance table ("Delete this service record?"). The server then **recalculates** the equipment's due date from the most recent remaining service record.

### 3.6 Monitor maintenance due dates and alerts

- The **dashboard** shows the number of **active alerts** and, lower down, the list of alerts with their priority and due date.
- To query due dates precisely, use the **AI assistant** ("Which equipment has maintenance or an inspection due soon?").
- **There is no button** to generate or handle alerts from the screen. The automatic generation (maintenance/inspection within 7 days, insurance within 30 days) and the "handled" flag exist on the server side (see 4.6 and 4.11) but are not exposed to the user.

### 3.7 Add a vehicle to the fleet

1. **Vehicles** tab → **"New vehicle"**.
2. Enter the **license plate** (required, unique); as needed the make, model, year (1900-2100), type, capacity + unit, initial mileage, fuel consumption, cost per km, and notes.
3. **Create**. Initial status `disponible`.

### 3.8 Update a vehicle's status or mileage

1. **Vehicles** tab → **Status** column: inline dropdown (disponible / en_deplacement / maintenance / hors_service).
2. The mileage is updated via the API (`PUT /logistics/vehicles/{id}`). Only the status, mileage, and notes can be modified after creation; the other fields (make, type, capacity, etc.) are not editable — you must delete and recreate.

### 3.9 Schedule a site-coordination activity

1. **"Trips"** tab (which contains coordination) → **"New activity"**.
2. Enter the **date** and the **activity type** (both required); as needed the start and end times, the area concerned, the person in charge, and notes.
3. **Create**. Reference `COORD-#####`, status `planifie`.
4. Advance the activity via the inline status menu (planifie → en_cours → termine, or annule).

### 3.10 View the fleet in GPS tracking

1. **GPS / Trips** tab → **"GPS Vehicles"** sub-tab.
2. The table shows each vehicle's last known position (latitude, longitude, speed, timestamp). This is **read-only**: positions come from GPS tracking, not manual entry.
3. The **Geofences** and **Routes** sub-tabs are also read-only.

### 3.11 Add a GPS location

1. **GPS / Trips** tab → **"Locations"** sub-tab → **"Add location"**.
2. Enter the **name**, the **latitude**, and the **longitude** (required); as needed the type (default `chantier`) and the city.
3. **Add**. The location appears in the table. This is the **only** creation possible in the GPS tab.

### 3.12 Query the AI assistant

1. **AI Assistant** tab.
2. Click an example or type your question (for example: "How many equipment items are under maintenance and which ones?").
3. **Enter** to send (**Shift+Enter** for a new line). The assistant reads your actual data and answers; the cost appears under the answer.
4. Chain questions: the assistant takes the last 12 exchanges into account.

> If you see "AI credits exhausted" (402 error), top up the company's AI credit balance (Configuration / Subscription module). Logistics consultation itself remains free.

### 3.13 Understand a refusal (403) or consultation mode

- If a creation or a deletion returns **403** while you are logged in: your role is probably not on the write list (administrator, manager, foreman, storekeeper). Ask an administrator.
- If **all** writes fail for **everyone**: the tenant is in **consultation mode** (inactive Stripe subscription). Settle the subscription to restore writing.

### 4.8 AI assistant — parameters, guards, and cost (`logistique_ai.py`)

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Endpoint | `POST /api/erp/v1/logistique/ai/chat` |
| Model | `claude-sonnet-4-6` |
| Maximum answer length | 8,000 tokens |
| Tools | **a single one**: `recherche_bd` (a **read-only** SQL query) |
| Tool loop | up to 5 iterations per message |
| History kept | last 12 turns, truncated to 8,000 characters each |
| Injected context | a summary of the tenant's logistics data, capped at 8,000 characters |
| Language | Quebec French by default, English if the interface language is English |

**Tables readable by the assistant**: `logistics_deliveries`, `logistics_delivery_items`, `logistics_equipment`, `logistics_equipment_reservations`, `logistics_equipment_maintenance`, `logistics_vehicles`, `logistics_vehicle_trips`, `logistics_site_coordination`, `logistics_alerts`, plus `projects` and `companies`. **Off-limits tables** (refused by a filter): employees, payroll, salaries, SIN, users, AI credits, Stripe, tokens, etc. Schema-qualified references are refused (protection against accessing another tenant).

**Guards, in order**:

1. AI service not configured → **503**.
2. `check_ai_guard` AI access control → **neutral in practice**: it lets any authenticated user through (`ai.py:824-825`, "allow all authenticated users").
3. Credits exhausted → **402** "AI credits exhausted."

**Cost**: `(input_tokens × 0.003 + output_tokens × 0.015) ÷ 1000 × 1.30` (the model's rate **plus a 30% markup**). The charge is **firm** and the message is tracked under `logistique_chat` in `public.ai_usage_tracking`.

> **Two AI assistants exist in the code, only one is wired up.** The screen uses `logistique_ai.py` (`/logistique/ai/chat`, above). The four former endpoints `POST /logistics/ia/analyser`, `/chat`, `/rapport`, `/optimiser` (in `secondary.py`) do have client-side functions but **are called by no screen** — they are **dead**. Do not rely on them.

### 4.10 Write roles and permissions

- **Logistics write roles** (`LOGISTICS_WRITE_ROLES`, `secondary.py:44`): `admin`, `super_admin`, `gestionnaire`, `contremaitre`, `magasinier`. An account marked `is_admin` (right re-read on the server) is always authorized. Any other role attempting to write → **403**.
- **Reads**: open to any authenticated user of the tenant.
- **GPS writes** (`POST /gps/locations`, `POST /gps/geofences`): **no** role guard — any authenticated user can create a location or a zone (a known inconsistency relative to the logistics writes).
- **Consultation mode**: a tenant with no active Stripe subscription sees **all** its writes (including GPS) refused with **403**, reads remaining permitted.

### 4.11 Automatic numbers, alert and reservation rules

- **Reference numbers**: `LIV-#####`, `EQP-#####`, `COORD-#####`. The server draws a number and tests it against the **uniqueness** constraint (up to 8 attempts, then widens the entropy). The uniqueness constraint remains the final safeguard: two identical references can never exist.
- **Equipment reservation overlap**: when creating a reservation, the server places a lock on the equipment (with a maximum delay of 5 seconds) and checks that no non-cancelled reservation overlaps the requested period; otherwise it returns **409**. The period must have an end later than or equal to the start (otherwise **422**). This protection exists **at the server level**; there is no screen to trigger it.
- **Alert generation** (`POST /logistics/alerts/generate`, no active duplicate):

| Source | Condition | Alert type | Priority |
|--------|-----------|------------|----------|
| Equipment maintenance | `prochaine_maintenance` ≤ today + 7 days | `maintenance_prevue` | **high** if ≤ +2 days, otherwise `normale` |
| Equipment inspection | `prochaine_inspection` ≤ today + 7 days | `inspection_requise` | **high** if ≤ +2 days, otherwise `normale` |
| Vehicle insurance | `assurance_expiration` ≤ today + 30 days | `assurance_expiration` | **high** if ≤ +7 days, otherwise `normale` |

- **Maintenance propagation**: creating, modifying, or deleting a service record automatically updates or recalculates the equipment's `prochaine_maintenance` due date.

## 5. Integrations and FAQ

### 5.1 Projects and suppliers (weak references)

- Deliveries, equipment, reservations, trips, and activities can carry a `project_id` (and a delivery a `fournisseur_id`) **in the database**, but the interface **does not offer** a way to enter them. These are logical links to `projects.id` and `companies.id`, **without** a displayed join or cascade: deleting a project does not touch the logistics records.
- The AI assistant, for its part, can read the `projects` and `companies` tables to cross-reference your data.

### 5.2 Store / Inventory (Module 10)

- The `inventory_item_id` field of a delivery item can point to a Store product, but **there is no screen** to enter items, and **no stock update** is triggered when a delivery moves to "delivered."
- **Best practice**: after physical receipt, manually create an inbound movement in the Store (Module 10), citing the `LIV-#####` reference in the reason.

### 5.3 Maintenance (Module 22) — overlapping scope

- **Logistics → Equipment → Maintenance** covers the maintenance of the **logistics fleet's equipment** (cranes, excavators, trucks), with automatic propagation of the due date and feeding of the alerts.
- **Module 22 (Maintenance)** covers general site maintenance with richer service records.
- The two modules do not share the same tables. For a consolidated view, consult each module separately.

### 5.4 Rental (Module 21) — overlapping scope

- **Logistics → Equipment** declares the equipment **owned or rented by the company** (the "Ownership type" field) for internal use.
- **Module 21 (Rental)** manages **rental contracts** offered to clients or contracted from suppliers, with billing (GST/QST), deposits, and returns. Distinct tables.

### 5.5 GPS module

- The "GPS / Trips" tab consumes the `/gps/*` endpoints (same `gps_*` tables). The link between a fleet vehicle and its GPS trace is made through the `logistics_vehicles` table (shared by the two lists).
- **Two vehicle lists read the same table**: the Vehicles tab (a simple list) and the GPS Vehicles sub-tab (with the last position). This is normal redundancy, not duplicated data.

### 5.6 AI credits (Module 25)

- The logistics assistant shares the **same AI credit wallet** as the ERP's other assistants.
- Each message is tracked under the `logistique_chat` feature, visible in the super administrator's AI usage tracking.

### 5.7 Frequently asked questions

**Can I generate a PDF delivery note?**
No. The module has no export or printing. No PDF, no CSV.

**How do I change the type or the area of a delivery that has already been created?**
It is not possible from the interface: only the **status** (inline menu) and **deletion** are offered. Delete and recreate the delivery. Same rule for equipment, vehicles, and activities.

**Where are a delivery's detailed items?**
There is no screen for them. The server can store them (description, quantities, unit, compliance), but no interface lets you enter or view them.

**How do I reserve a piece of equipment for a project?**
Not from the interface. Reservations (with overlap refusal) exist on the server side but have no screen. Workaround: set the equipment to the "Reserved" status or record the period in the notes.

**Does the module prevent two reservations on the same dates?**
Yes, **on the server side**: a reservation that overlaps another is refused (409 error). But since there is no reservation screen, this protection is not visible in use.

**How do I record a vehicle trip (departure, return, fuel)?**
No screen. Despite the "Trips" and "GPS / Trips" tabs, vehicle trips are not exposed.

**Why does the "Trips" tab show site coordination and not trips?**
It is a label inherited from an older design. The "Trips" tab manages **site coordination**; the "GPS / Trips" tab manages the **GPS fleet**. The real vehicle-trip concept has no tab.

**Is there a map of the vehicles?**
No. The GPS tab displays latitudes and longitudes as text, never an interactive map.

**How do I generate or handle an alert?**
The dashboard displays alerts read-only. There is **no button** to generate them or mark them handled. The generation (maintenance/inspection within 7 days, insurance within 30 days) and the handling exist on the server side but are not exposed.

**Does a "delivered" delivery update the stock in the Store?**
No. No synchronization. Create the inbound movement manually in the Store.

**Do costs (daily, fuel, maintenance) feed accounting?**
No. They are displayed only, never turned into accounting entries.

**A brand-new tenant shows counters at zero: is this normal?**
Yes. The `logistics_*` tables are created on the first entry. As soon as you create a first delivery or a first piece of equipment, the counters fill in.

**The GPS tab returns an error on an account that has never used GPS. Why?**
Because `gps.py` does not create its tables on demand. The `gps_*` tables must exist (GPS data already received) for the tab to respond.

**Who can create and delete records?**
The administrator, super administrator, manager, foreman, and storekeeper roles (or any `is_admin` account). Other roles get a 403 error. Note: adding GPS locations and zones has **no** role restriction.

**Can the AI assistant see payroll or employees?**
No. It reads only the logistics tables, plus projects and companies. Payroll, employees, salaries, and SIN are off-limits to it, and it never writes.

**How much does a question to the assistant cost?**
The `claude-sonnet-4-6` model's rate plus a 30% markup, charged to your AI credits. The exact cost is shown under each answer. Logistics consultation itself is free.

## 6. Summary

- **Purpose**: an operational register of site logistics — deliveries, equipment (+ maintenance), vehicles, coordination, GPS fleet tracking (read-only) — and a read-only AI assistant.
- **Access**: sidebar → **FIELD** group → **Logistics** (`Truck` icon), route `/logistique`. Default tab: Dashboard.
- **7 tabs**: Dashboard, Deliveries, Equipment, Vehicles, **"Trips"** (= site coordination), **"GPS / Trips"** (= GPS fleet), AI Assistant. Two tabs carry the word "Trips," neither one manages vehicle trips.
- **3 server-side routers**: `secondary.py` (`/logistics/*`, 33 management endpoints + statistics, plus 4 **dead** AI endpoints), `gps.py` (`/gps/*`, 7 endpoints), `logistique_ai.py` (`/logistique/ai/chat`, the active assistant). There is **no** `routers/logistique.py` file.
- **Tables**: `logistics_*` per tenant, **created on demand** (a new tenant sees counters at zero); `gps_*` per tenant, **not created on demand** (a possible error on a tenant with no GPS data).
- **Permissions**: reading open to all; writing reserved for the administrator / super administrator / manager / foreman / storekeeper roles (or `is_admin`); GPS writes have **no** role guard; consultation mode (read-only) if the Stripe subscription is inactive.
- **Limited editing**: on an existing record, you can only **change the status** (inline menu) and **delete**. No full-edit window.
- **Numbers**: `LIV-#####`, `EQP-#####`, `COORD-#####`, guaranteed unique by a database constraint.
- **Alerts**: three sources (maintenance and inspection within 7 days, insurance within 30 days), high priority according to imminence — generated and handled **on the server side only**, displayed read-only on the dashboard.
- **Equipment reservations**: overlap refusal (409) **on the server side**, with no screen.
- **AI assistant**: a **read-only** chat, `claude-sonnet-4-6` model, 8,000 tokens, single tool `recherche_bd`, sensitive tables off-limits, **firm** charge of AI credits (the model's rate plus a 30% markup, `logistique_chat` feature). The real gatekeeper is the credit balance (402); `check_ai_guard` is neutral.
- **What the module does not do**: no export / printing / CSV / PDF / upload / bulk action; no delivery items, reservations, vehicle trips, GPS zone creation, or alert generation **in the interface**; no map; no notifications; no stock or accounting synchronization; no assistant that writes.
